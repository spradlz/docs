> **ZACH (8/18):** *"like building a skyscraper: we start at the foundation and build our way up, and we have 90 days to finish this skyscraper."*

**THE RULES:** one floor per section · weeks build floors in batches · no floor starts until the one below it is GATED · machine gates clear autonomously, **ZACH GATES** only for design locks, real-human sends, destructive ops, and laws.

**THE CHANGE-ORDER LAW (Zach 8/18):** *"whenever i add items and we find stuff and i throw random things in, it has to fit into the existing blueprint and the existing floor — like a change order. it can't just be amended to the end of the build; it has to fit into where it fits in the project."* Every new item — Zach's asks, walk finds, bug discoveries — is FILED ONTO ITS FLOOR at intake, never appended to the end. If it lands on a floor already gated, that floor REOPENS (honestly, with the gate re-run) rather than the item drifting to the top of the tower. An item that fits no floor is a scope question for Zach, not a floor-29.

**TOP-OUT (the MVP):** 50K leads in FUB · values on all 1,800 past-client homes · every lead on a Myli cohort plan · dashboards perfect · Myli learning, engaging, booking tours.
