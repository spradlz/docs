> **ZACH (8/18):** *"like building a skyscraper: we start at the foundation and build our way up, and we have 90 days to finish this skyscraper."*

**THE RULES:** one floor per section · weeks build floors in batches · no floor starts until the one below it is GATED · machine gates clear autonomously, **ZACH GATES** only for design locks, real-human sends, destructive ops, and laws.

**THE CHANGE-ORDER LAW (Zach 8/18):** *"whenever i add items and we find stuff and i throw random things in, it has to fit into the existing blueprint and the existing floor — like a change order. it can't just be amended to the end of the build; it has to fit into where it fits in the project."* Every new item is FILED ONTO ITS FLOOR at intake, never appended to the end. If it lands on a gated floor, that floor REOPENS honestly. An item that fits no floor is a scope question for Zach, not a floor-29.

**TOP-OUT (the MVP):** 50K leads in FUB · values on all 1,800 past-client homes · every lead on a Myli cohort plan · dashboards perfect · Myli learning, engaging, booking tours.

## How to read the numbers

- **Floor 1–28** = a floor of this building. Floors live in this table and in the section headers — nowhere else.
- **№ 1–190** = an item's BUILD ORDER on [THE MASTER CHECKLIST](/master-plan/master-checklist).
- **[FUB-8]** = "item 8 on the FUB Pipeline board" — the bracket tag names the item's **home board** (FUB / BED / SELL / TASK) where its receipts live. **It is never a floor number.**
