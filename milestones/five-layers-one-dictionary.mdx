# ⭐ Five Layers, One Dictionary

**Milestone completed August 22, 2026.** [Floor 9 on the board](/master-plan/floor-9)

---

## What this is

Three pieces of tidying under the foundation, all versions of the same principle: **one fact should
live in exactly one place.**

That principle sounds like housekeeping. It is not. When a fact has two homes, you get two answers to
one question, and no reliable way to tell which one a given screen is showing you. You find out the
hard way - a number on one page disagreeing with a number on another, and a day spent working out
which one lied.

## The walkthrough

### One: a lead's stage had two homes, and only one was the truth

Every lead has a stage - new, contacted, under contract. That stage was being stored in two different
places. One of them was real. The other was a leftover.

We measured it before touching anything, which turned out to matter: **nothing in the live system read
the leftover, and nothing wrote to it** - but **130 of the 147 leads were still carrying stale values
in it.** A field full of confident, out-of-date answers that nobody was consulting. It only takes one
new screen reading the wrong one for those old answers to surface as though they were current.

It is gone. And it is **guarded** - there is a standing check that fails loudly if the twin ever comes
back, with the failure message spelling out what went wrong. The guard exists because a thing you
deleted once will be recreated by somebody who did not know, unless something is standing there
saying no.

### Two: the record of changes was pointing at things that did not exist

Every change to the shape of the database gets written into a ledger. Twenty-four entries in that
ledger pointed at nothing at all.

Reconciled, they turned out to be four different problems wearing one label. Eighteen were duplicates
and renumbering debris - the same change recorded twice, under two slightly different names - cleared
with a backup taken first. Six were genuine gaps worth chasing.

The headline is the reassuring half: **nothing in the code was sitting there waiting to run.** Every
discrepancy was on the other side of the ledger - the record claiming something the code could not
show - which is a bookkeeping problem. The other direction, a change written but never applied, is
the one that bites, and there were none.

### Three: a relationship can now end instead of being erased

This is the most human of the three.

Two people are connected in the system - partners, a buyer and their co-buyer, whatever it is. Then
they are not. Disconnecting them used to **delete the fact that they had ever been connected at all.**
The history did not just stop, it had never happened.

That is wrong in a way anybody can feel. People's lives change, and the fact that two people bought a
house together in 2019 does not stop being true because they are no longer together in 2026. Your
records should be able to say "this was true, from then until then."

Disconnecting now **ends the stint** and keeps the history. Reconnect them later and it opens a new
one, sitting after the old one, and the ended stint stays on the record instead of vanishing.

One honest limit, because it matters more than the win: the record keeps the ended stint, but **no
screen shows it to you yet.** Every one of the fourteen places that read connections deliberately
shows only the live ones - and that filtering is the point, because an ended relationship must never
quietly carry on receiving saved-search mail or home-value reports. So the history is *retained and
can be asked of the record*, not *visible on the page*. Surfacing it is still to come.

## What you can do with it

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Read a lead's stage | One answer, from one place, with no stale twin behind it |
| Try to reintroduce the twin | A standing check fails loudly and names what went wrong |
| Disconnect two people | The relationship ends and the history stays - it is not erased |
| Reconnect them later | A new stint opens, and the ended one is kept on the record rather than erased |
| Look at the record of changes | Every entry points at something real, and nothing is silently waiting to run |
