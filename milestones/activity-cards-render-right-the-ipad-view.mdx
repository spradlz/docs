# ⭐ Activity Cards Render Right

**Milestone completed August 23, 2026.** [Floor 29 on the board](/master-plan/floor-29)

---

## What this is

Open anybody's record and you get a running list of everything that has ever happened to them. Each
entry is a **card**. A card is supposed to read like a sentence a person would say out loud:

> **Conner Testlead sent an email** - to info@houseloop.ai

Two things were breaking that. Nearly four hundred email cards were showing the **subject line where
the person's name belonged**, because the sentence was being built before we knew who sent it. And
text scraped off the furniture of a web page - buttons, headings, navigation - was being written down
as though somebody had actually said it.

Both are fixed, and fixed in the one place that builds every card rather than on each screen
separately. That distinction matters: a fix applied per screen is a fix that comes undone the next
time somebody adds a screen.

## The written detail

- [The activity card catalog](/command-center/activity-card-catalog) - the signed grammar every card
  is built to: what a card is made of, how it is coloured, and the no-truncation rule.
- [The lead detail guide](/guides/lead-detail) - the record itself, laid out piece by piece.

## The walkthrough

### Before: the audit that counted the damage

We did not start by fixing cards. We started by auditing all one hundred and eighty-eight kinds of
card, one at a time, and writing down what was wrong with each.

![The card audit - one hundred and eighty-eight kinds, with one hundred and thirty-two rendering grey and empty, thirty-five in the wrong colour and fifteen with broken grammar](/images/milestones/activity-cards-render-right-the-ipad-view/before-the-card-audit.png)

One hundred and thirty-two of them rendered grey with an empty icon. Thirty-five were the wrong
colour. Fifteen had broken grammar. The cause was a hand-kept list of seventy-two entries that
somebody had to remember to update - so of course it drifted.

### After: open a record and read it

![A lead's record where every card leads with the person's name - Conner Testlead sent an email, Jasmeet Kaur sent a text](/images/milestones/activity-cards-render-right-the-ipad-view/after-cards-name-the-actor.png)

**Conner Testlead sent an email** - the name is the headline, and the subject sits underneath where a
subject belongs. **Jasmeet Kaur-TEST sent a text** - with the message quoted, and a footnote saying
whose record it also appears on.

### One card, close up

![A single activity card reading Zach Spradling added two homes to their dashboard](/images/milestones/activity-cards-render-right-the-ipad-view/one-card-close-up.png)

*Zach Spradling added 2 homes to their dashboard.* Somebody, something they did, and the detail. That
is the entire grammar, and every card in the system is held to it.

## What you can do with it

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Open any lead's record | A time-ordered list of everything that has ever happened to that person |
| Read any card | A named person and a real sentence - never a subject line standing in for a name |
| Use the tabs across the top | The same list narrowed to texts, emails, calls, or inquiries |
| Look at a card's colour and icon | Both come from the one catalog, so the same kind of thing always looks the same |
| Find a card about a group message | A footnote tells you whose other records it appears on |

## What was actually fixed

- **Email cards said who sent them.** The sentence used to be assembled before the sender's name was
  known, so it fell back to the subject line. Nearly four hundred cards were affected.
- **Page furniture stopped being filed as speech.** New ones are blocked at the moment of capture,
  and the ones already written down were cleaned up.
- **The fix went in the factory, not the showroom.** One card builder, one catalog. Add a new kind of
  card and it inherits the grammar instead of needing its own patch.
