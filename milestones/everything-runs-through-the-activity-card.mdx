# ⭐ Everything Runs Through the Activity Card

**Milestone completed August 23, 2026.** [Floor 30 on the board](/master-plan/floor-30)

---

## What this is

A lead's record has three lanes. The person on the left, Myli on the right, and in the middle the
**activity card** - the thing you actually work from.

Everything you do to a lead starts on that card. Text them, email them, call them, leave a note, set
a task: same place, same card, no second screen. And underneath it, the running history of everything
that has happened, in order.

The question this milestone closed was a suspicion that some of the buttons on that card did nothing
when you pressed them. We measured it rather than assuming. The answer turned out to be more
interesting than the question.

## The walkthrough

### You open a lead

![A lead's record in three lanes - the person on the left, the activity card in the middle with tabs for text, email, call, note and task, and Myli on the right](/images/milestones/everything-runs-through-the-activity-card/the-whole-lead-in-one-screen.png)

Left lane: who they are, their homes, their tags, their stage. Middle: the card, with a row of tabs -
**+Text, +Email, +Call, +Note, +Task** - and the history below it. Right lane: Myli, watching.

The card at the bottom of that feed reads *"Zach Spradling sent a text to Conner Kelley-TEST - group
text with Jasmeet Kaur-TEST, replies visible to all."* One sentence that tells you who, what, to
whom, and that it was a group.

### You click the Email tab

![The email tab open on the activity card, with To, From and Subject fields and a row of template chips](/images/milestones/everything-runs-through-the-activity-card/the-email-tab.png)

To, from, subject, body - and a row of templates along the top: **Introduction, Follow Up, Still
Buying, Nurture Lead, Custom**. Pick one, it fills the body, you edit it and send. You never left the
lead's record, and the email you sent lands in the same feed underneath.

### The buttons that supposedly did nothing

They had never been built. The record we were working from described a proposal somebody had written
down, not a bug somebody had found - so there was no dead button to fix, because there was no button.
The real gap behind the suspicion had already been closed by other work.

We wrote that down rather than quietly ticking the box, because "we checked and there was nothing
there" is a different fact from "we fixed it," and the difference matters the next time somebody
reads the record.

## What you can do with it

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Click **+Text** | Compose and send a text without leaving the record; it appears in the feed below |
| Click **+Email** | To, from, subject and body, with your templates one click away |
| Click **+Call** | It rings your mobile first, connects you, and logs the call into the same feed |
| Click **+Note** | A note on the record, stamped with who wrote it |
| Click **+Task** | A task with a due date, which becomes Myli's clock |
| Use the tabs above the feed | The same history narrowed to All, AI, Inquiries, Messages or Calls |
