# ⭐ Notifications Work Properly

**Milestone completed August 23, 2026.** [Floor 32 on the board](/master-plan/floor-32)

---

## What this is

Three separate things were wrong with notifications, and one of them had been wrong for weeks without
anybody being able to see it.

The list would not page. The system kept emailing people whose lead had been thrown in the trash. And
underneath both, a switch that appears on no settings page anywhere was quietly turning away roughly
nineteen hundred notifications a month, while the settings page you actually look at cheerfully
described decisions it was not making.

All three are closed. The third one is the reason this milestone matters more than it sounds.

## The written detail

- [The notifications plan](/master-plan/notifications) - per-seat rules: who gets told, about what,
  on which channel, for which leads, decided in exactly one place.
- [The containment law](/command-center/ai-containment-law) - "every toggle must actually gate." A
  switch that reads *off* while messages still leave is the defect this milestone exists to kill.
- [The end-to-end verification ledger](/master-plan/e2e-verification) - the item-by-item receipts.

## The walkthrough

### Before: a count that could not be true

![The notifications page reading one hundred in the header while the tab beside it reads one hundred and fifty-two unread](/images/milestones/notifications-work-properly/before-100-vs-152.png)

The header said **100 notifications**. The tab next to it said **152 unread**. You cannot have more
unread things than things. The list was capped at a single page, so the header was counting the page
rather than the pile - and every page after the first quietly served you the first page again.

### After: the whole pile, paging properly

![The notifications page reading one thousand three hundred and thirty-seven, with two hundred and eighty-six unread](/images/milestones/notifications-work-properly/after-1337-paging.png)

**1,337**, with **286** genuinely unread. The count was right all along - paging was the bug. We
walked it from page one through to the end rather than checking the first screen and calling it done.

### The switches, and what each one says it does

![Notification settings showing the global delivery switches, with email notifications off and saying nothing sends](/images/milestones/notifications-work-properly/the-switches.png)

The master switches at the top say their own consequence out loud: **Email notifications · OFF -
nothing sends**. Not a bare toggle you have to interpret. Underneath, *which leads notify me* - only
the ones assigned to you, or every lead any agent touches.

### Event by event, and who is allowed to change it

![The per-event notification matrix, with lock icons on rows where texting is off by team policy](/images/milestones/notifications-work-properly/the-per-event-matrix.png)

Every event gets a row and every channel gets a column, with the real number of times each has been
sent. Where your team has made a decision for everybody, the row is locked and says why: *"Texting is
off for this event - team policy."* The column an administrator controls is greyed out for everyone
else rather than hidden, so you can see the rule exists even when you cannot change it.

## What you can do with it

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Open Notifications | The whole list, correctly counted, newest first |
| Click **Unread** | Only the ones you have not seen - and the number matches the rows |
| Scroll to page two | Genuinely different rows, not the first page again |
| Turn off a channel | It says what that means: nothing sends on that channel |
| Choose which leads notify you | Only yours, or every lead any agent touches |
| Open the per-event matrix | Every event, every channel, and how many times each has fired |
| Find a locked row | Your team set that one for everybody, and the row tells you so |
| Open the run log tab | Every notification that fired: when, which lead, what it was, which channels it tried, whether it was seen |

## What was actually fixed

- **Paging.** The count was right; every page after the first served the same rows again. Walked from
  page one to the end.
- **The trashed-lead leak.** Four different roads could throw a lead in the trash and only some of
  them stopped the email. They all go through one door now, and both gates hold.
- **The switch nobody could see.** The measurement nobody had made: the designed per-seat rules turned
  away **7** notifications in a month, while an emergency switch that appears on no settings page
  turned away **1,907** across thirty-four kinds of event in the same window. Everything on the
  settings page was describing a decision being made somewhere you could not look.
