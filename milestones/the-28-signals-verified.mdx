# ⭐ The 28 Signals Verified

**Milestone completed August 19, 2026.** [Floor 1 on the board](/master-plan/floor-1)

---

## What this is

Follow Up Boss is the pipe every Zillow lead comes to us through. When one of your buyers views a
home, asks for a tour, gets a text, or fills out a form, Follow Up Boss tells us about it. There are
twenty-eight different things it can tell us. We call each one a **signal**.

For a long time we believed all twenty-eight worked, because nobody had ever sat down and checked.

This milestone is the checking. We created twenty real records inside Follow Up Boss's own system -
so Follow Up Boss pushed every message to us, exactly the way it does for a real client, with nothing
faked at our end - and then we watched each one land and wrote down a verdict. Eight were fired live
in front of us. The other eighteen were confirmed against real traffic that had already arrived that
week from real people.

Every one of the twenty-eight now has a written verdict next to its name. Four of them came back
wrong, and all four were fixed the same week.

## The walkthrough

### You open the CRM and go to the triggers page

Every signal Follow Up Boss can send is a row. The toggle on the left says whether we are listening.
The number on the right is how many times it has actually fired.

![The Follow Up Boss signals, each one a row with its own toggle and its own firing count](/images/milestones/the-28-signals-verified/fub-signals-live.png)

Nothing here is a guess. A row exists because the system really carries that signal, and the count
next to it is the real number of times it has arrived.

### You click a signal, and it shows you every time it has ever fired

This is the part that turned "we think it works" into "here is the proof." Click **Tour Requested**
and you get its whole history - eighty-seven of them, newest first, each stamped with the date, the
person, and the road it came in on.

![The firing log for Tour Requested, showing eighty-seven firings with the date, the person and the road for each](/images/milestones/the-28-signals-verified/the-firing-log.png)

Two names in the middle of that list are blurred because they are real clients and this page is
shared. Everything else you see is a test person we created on purpose.

### The signal arrives, and it becomes a card on the person's record

The end of the road. Somebody messages you on Zillow, and it shows up on their record in plain
English - who it was, what they did, and which home they were looking at.

![A lead's record showing a Zillow in-app message, a property view and a saved search, each one arriving from Follow Up Boss](/images/milestones/the-28-signals-verified/signal-arrives-as-a-card.png)

"Viewed a property on Zillow" with the address. "Sent a Zillow in-app message" with the message in
it. "Created a saved search." That is the whole point of the pipe: the thing that happened over
there becomes a sentence you can read over here.

## What you can do with it

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Open the triggers page | You see all twenty-eight signals, with the ones we are listening to switched on |
| Click any signal | Its complete firing history opens - every time it has arrived, newest first |
| Read the count beside a signal | That is the real number of times it has fired, not an estimate |
| Switch a signal off | We stop reacting to it, and the page says so |
| Open any lead's record | Everything Follow Up Boss has told us about that person is there, in order, in plain sentences |

## What the firing squad found

Four defects, all fixed the same week:

- **A second spelling was dropping a signal on the floor.** The record held two ways of writing the
  same event and we only recognised the longer one, so the short one vanished every time.
- **Million-dollar prices were falling out of the note reader.** It understood a price written out in
  full but not one written in shorthand - so our most expensive leads were being filed as plain notes.
- **A note that produced nothing used to disappear silently.** Now it raises a loud error naming what
  it could not read, instead of quietly doing nothing.
- **Two roads carrying the same fact were making two cards.** They share one card maker now, so
  whichever arrives first, you get one card.
