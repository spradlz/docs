# ⭐ The Leads Table Filters Like a Human

**Milestone completed August 23, 2026.** [Floor 31 on the board](/master-plan/floor-31)

---

## What this is

The leads page had a quiet, corrosive bug: the big number in the header and the table sitting
underneath it were counting two different things. The header counted everybody, ever. The table
showed the last thirty days.

Neither number was wrong on its own. Put next to each other, they made the page a liar - and once a
page has lied to you about a count, you stop trusting every count on it.

They read one rule now. Whatever the header says, that is how many rows are underneath it.

## The walkthrough

### You open Leads and pick one filter

Stage includes **New Lead**.

![The leads page with one filter applied - the header reads 47 and the line underneath reads 47 people, one filter active](/images/milestones/leads-table-works-filters-like-a-human/one-filter-47.png)

The header chip says **47**. The line under the filter builder says **47 people · 1 filter active**.
The chip below that spells the filter back to you in English: *Stage: New Lead*.

### You stack a second filter

Has a phone number, **and** created in the last thirty days.

![The same page with two filters stacked - has phone, created less than thirty days ago - and the count now reads 55 everywhere](/images/milestones/leads-table-works-filters-like-a-human/two-filters-55.png)

Header **55**. Line underneath, **55 people · 2 filters active**. Both filters listed back to you as
chips you can click to remove. The two conditions combine the way you would expect - people who have
a phone *and* arrived recently - not one replacing the other.

### The saved views along the top

Not Contacted **9**. Request Needs Reply **19**. Response Needed **18**. Task Overdue **6**. Those
counts are live, and they are counted by the same rule as everything else on the page.

## What you can do with it

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Open Leads | Every lead assigned to you, or where you are a collaborator |
| Click a saved view | The list narrows, and the count in the header follows it |
| Add a filter in the builder | Pick a field, pick a comparison, pick a value - it applies immediately |
| Stack a second filter | Conditions combine; both are spelled back to you as chips |
| Click the × on a chip | That one condition comes off, the rest stay |
| **Save as Smart Filter** | The combination becomes a saved view of your own, with its own live count |
| Read any count on the page | Header, "people" line and table all count the same thing |
