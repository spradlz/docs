## What it will be

A first-class tool in the loop, the buyer-side counterpart to the
[Listing Presentation](/listing-presentation/overview) tool on the seller side. Today, writing an offer is
a manual sequence: pull the MLS data, create and name the loop, apply the template, upload and rename the
documents, fill Loop Details, then fill the forms. Every step is documented, repeatable, and done by hand.

The tool collapses that into: enter the offer terms, and the package is assembled, filled and sent.
