## 📌 Summary

**Every document a buyer signs lives on this ONE page.** Zach 2026-08-07: *"I think we should have
playbooks — buyer document signing playbook and seller document signing playbook — and have every
document in one section for a buyer with the description, the folder it's in, and the instruction."*

The page is organised by **loop folder**, because that is where an agent actually looks for a document.
Each folder gets an index, then one entry per document: **what it is · the folder · the instruction.**

Seller-side documents live on the [Seller Document Signing Playbook](/tc/playbook-seller-documents).

<Note>
**WHY ONE PAGE AND NOT ONE PAGE PER FORM.** We are heading for ~58 templates (lead-based paint, right to
show, addenda…). Fifty-eight pages that each say "fill it and send it" is sprawl, and it buries the three
documents that actually carry a rule someone can get wrong. A form earns a *section*, not a *page*.
</Note>

## 📁 Showing & Touring Agreements

**The buyer-representation agreements — signed BEFORE showing property.** Folder label:
`Showing & Touring Agreements` (categories `buyer_agency`, `buyer_disclosure`).

| Document | Pages | Rides with it | Signed by | Send when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Exclusive Buyer Agency Contract** | 6 (**9-page packet**) | KS + MO disclosures | buyer · co-buyer · designated agent (broker line for MO) | The standard path — before showing any property |
| **Single Property Buyer-Broker Rep** | 4 | — none | buyer · co-buyer · agent · broker | Buyer will only be shown ONE specific house |
| **Non-Exclusive Buyer-Tenant Broker Rep** | 6 | — none | buyer · co-buyer · agent · broker | Buyer will not sign exclusive; not our standard path |
| **Kansas Brokerage Relationships** | 1 | — (rides WITH the agency) | buyer · co-buyer | Never alone — attached to the Exclusive agreement |
| **Missouri Broker Disclosure** | 2 | — (rides WITH the agency) | buyer · co-buyer | Never alone — attached to the Exclusive agreement |

### Exclusive Buyer Agency Contract

**What it is.** The representation agreement itself — the document that makes us the buyer's agent. It
names the client, the brokerage (eXp Realty LLC), the **designated agent**, the term, the property type
and the price range, and how we are compensated. The buyer initials pages 1–5 and signs page 6.

**Folder.** `Showing & Touring Agreements`

**The instruction.**
1. Send it **before showing property.** Everything downstream — offers, the transaction, the loop —
   assumes this exists.
2. It goes out as a **9-page packet**: the 6-page agreement welded to the Kansas and Missouri
   disclosures, one PDF, one signing session. Never send the agreement by itself.
3. Set the **designated agent** — that is who is licensed for this client, and it drives the licensing
   check below.
4. Leave the **broker unchecked** unless Missouri requires him. An unchecked party is not on the
   document and receives nothing.

<Warning>
**Ana Spradling, 2026-07-30:** *"I will need you to include **with the buyers agency** the Kansas
brokerage disclosures and the MO broker disclosure with it. **Per law they have to sign with the
agency.**"*

**Only the Exclusive Buyer Agency carries them.** BOTH disclosures ride along — never one state or the
other, because our market crosses the state line and nobody knows yet which side the house will be on.
</Warning>

### Single Property Buyer-Broker Representation Agreement

**What it is.** A representation agreement scoped to **ONE named house.** We represent the buyer for
that property and nothing else. Used when a buyer will not commit to exclusive representation but wants
to see a specific home.

**Folder.** `Showing & Touring Agreements`

**The instruction.**
1. It requires a **property address** — the form prints it, and the loop is always cut fresh for that
   house so the address is unambiguous.
2. **4 pages. No disclosures attached.** Everything signs on page 4.
3. If the same buyer later goes exclusive, that is a **new agreement in a new loop** — this one does not
   convert.

### Non-Exclusive Buyer-Tenant Broker Representation Agreement

**What it is.** Client-level representation without exclusivity — the buyer may work with other agents.
Covers buying **or leasing**. It exists; it is not the standard Spradling path.

**Folder.** `Showing & Touring Agreements`

**The instruction.**
1. **6 pages. No disclosures attached.** No property address — it is about the client, not a house.
2. Prefer the **Exclusive** agreement. Reach for this only when the buyer has refused exclusivity.

### Kansas Real Estate Brokerage Relationships

**What it is.** A **Kansas-required consumer notice**, not an agreement. It explains the relationships a
Kansas licensee may have — seller's agent, buyer's agent, transaction broker — so the client understands
which one we are. The client signs to acknowledge receipt. It grants nothing and obligates nobody.

**Folder.** `Showing & Touring Agreements` — always as part of the Buyer Agency packet.

**The instruction.** **Never send it alone.** It is welded to the Exclusive Buyer Agency and merges into
the same signing session. The form prints **no date line**, so no date field is placed — the signing
certificate at the back of the packet carries the timestamp.

### Missouri Broker Disclosure

**What it is.** Missouri's equivalent consumer notice. Sent alongside the Kansas one for the same reason:
the eventual house may be on either side of the state line, and that is unknown when the agency is signed.

**Folder.** `Showing & Touring Agreements` — always as part of the Buyer Agency packet.

**The instruction.** Same as Kansas — **never alone**, 2 pages, merged into the packet, **no printed date
line** so no date field is placed.

## The rules that apply to every buyer document

**The packet is the unit, not the form.** A document with attachments merges into ONE PDF and ONE signing
session. Three envelopes means three chances for one to sit unsigned.

**Unchecked means off the document.** A party you uncheck in the send modal is not a signer, is not on
the share list, and receives nothing — not the request, not the executed copy.

**We add nothing to the signed PDF.** Documenso's sealed output is archived byte-for-byte: its
signatures, its initials, its date fields, and the **signing certificate** it appends as the last page.
The verification stamp we used to draw was removed 2026-08-06 — it had nowhere to fit on forms with no
date column, and it does not scale to 58 templates.

**A missing date line is not a missing date field.** Where a form prints no "(Date)" caption, no date
field is placed — the extractor refuses to invent a coordinate (`SIGNATURE_ANCHORING.md`). The signing
certificate carries the timestamp for those pages.

**The licensing check warns, never blocks.** Zach holds a Kansas licence only and cannot be designated on
Missouri property. A mismatch is surfaced on the send result; the send still goes.
