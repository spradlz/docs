## 📌 Summary — what is true today

Every listing surface (the `/listings` grid, home shelves, saved-search previews, the search dropdown, the detail hero, the map) shows the same small set of **pills**. The bug we are killing: the pill was hand-rolled in ~12 files, so colors drifted and meant opposite things by surface (green = *For Sale* on a card but *Coming Soon* on the map), the count was inconsistent (1, 2, or 3), and "New" stacked with "Coming Soon."

**The lock (Zach 2026-08-08):**

- **ONE component, ONE color source.** Every pill renders through `components/listing/StatusPill.tsx`; every color comes from `lib/listings/pill-class.ts`. No surface hand-rolls a pill. Change a color once, there, forever.
- **The pill object is always the same:** white background · black text · one colored dot. Only the dot color changes (Coming Soon = white pill, no dot).
- **EXACTLY ONE pill (Zach 2026-08-08 FINAL — supersedes the old "max 2").** A card shows one white pill, chosen by priority: Sold → Under Contract → On Market → New (under 24h) → Open House → Price Cut → a Feature → days-on-market → For Sale. The DOT carries the signal; days + features are dotless white facts. No stacking, ever.
- **New and Coming Soon are mutually exclusive** — Coming Soon wins (shows "On Market" + the date).

## The color law — ALL WHITE PILLS, the DOT carries the meaning (Zach 2026-08-08, LOCKED)

**Every status pill is a WHITE pill with black text and ONE colored dot.** No solid-color status pills anywhere. Only the dot color changes.

| Status | Dot | Label |
|---|---|---|
| **New** (first 24h) | 🟢 green | "New · 1 hr ago" |
| **For Sale** (active) | 🟢 green | "For Sale" |
| **On Market** (was "Coming Soon") | 🟢 green | **"On Market Aug 13"** — the date in black, never the words "Coming Soon" |
| **Under Contract** (pending) | 🟡 **yellow** | "Under Contract" |
| **Sold** | 🔴 **red** | "Sold 8/7/26" (top-left, with the date) · near the price: red dot + "Sold" |
| **Off Market** | ⚫ grey | "Off Market" |

**The one non-dot pill — Price Cut:** a **RED pill above the price** ("Price cut $6,000 · 8/4"). A money signal, Zillow-style — the only colored pill, and it lives by the price, not on the photo.

**Facts** (Built 2023 · 3-Car Garage · Kitchen Island · **acreage** · price drop) render as plain gray feature chips — same size, **no colored dot**. A fact never wears a status color (that's why acreage lost its stray green dot).

## The count law — EXACTLY ONE pill, by priority (`pickOnePill`)

One white pill per card. The FIRST match wins — everything below it is dropped:

1. **Sold** (red dot) → **Under Contract** (yellow) → **On Market · date** (green) → **New · Xh** (green, under 24h)
2. **Open House** (blue dot)
3. **Price Cut · $X** (red dot)
4. **A Feature** (no dot — a plain white fact chip)
5. **X days on market** (no dot — the Zillow default when nothing above applies)
6. **For Sale** (green dot — ultimate fallback)

Source: `pickOnePill(listing)` in `ListingCard.tsx`. **Never in a pill:** beds, baths — those live in the card body; acreage is a fact in the facts line, not a pill.

## "Days on market" is computed from the listed DATE — never the feed's `daysOnMarket` column

The days pill (priority 5 above) and the calendar line on the card both derive their number from the
home's **MLS listed date**. Neither reads `Listing.daysOnMarket`, the value the Repliers feed ships and
we store at ingest. That column is not a clock, and — the part that fooled us for months — it is not a
*stale* clock either.

Measured on the live web database on 2026-09-08, across 8,167 Residential active listings, grouped by
how recently each row was re-synced from the feed:

| last re-synced | rows | off by >30 days | column reads 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| under 2 days ago | 7,967 | 3,162 | 1,428 |
| over 30 days ago | 102 | 102 | 28 |

Almost the whole estate is rewritten **daily** and still wrong, so syncing more often fixes nothing.
The clearest case: 8013 Millridge Street was re-synced that morning, had been listed 812 days earlier,
and its column read **0**. The value tracks no date we hold — it lands within two days of `mlsListedAt`
on 544 rows out of ~8,150, and on sold homes it usually turns out to be days-to-offer rather than
days-to-close.

**The rule.** One function owns the column: `daysOnMarketForRow` in `lib/listings/derive.ts`. It reads
the stored value, replaces it with a date-derived number, and every mapper that builds a `Listing`
(`dbRowToListing`, `historyRowToListing`, `readFromCache`) already runs through it. So
`listing.details.daysOnMarket` is the *derived* figure, and reading it is correct. What no surface may
do is reach past that function to the raw column — in SQL, or off a Prisma row.

The date it derives from, in order: `mlsListedAt`, then `listDate`, then nothing. A home with neither
renders **no** days figure at all; zero is never used as a stand-in for "unknown", because zero is a
sentence about the house and "we don't know" is not. On a sold or pending home the number freezes at
the list-to-sold (or list-to-offer) span rather than ticking forever.

Ingest still writes the column — the feed's own field stays on the tape for later audits. It simply
stops being read. `tools/vocab/dom-reader-census.mjs` is the ratchet that keeps it that way: it fails
the push if a new surface reads the raw column, and its floor only shrinks.

The market-level statistics built on this rule — median days on market versus median days to
contract, and the demand verdict that reads them — live on
[The /sell page](/website/sell-page).

## The map is its own medium (Zach 2026-08-08)

The map is not a card — it needs legibility, not the pill palette:

- **All markers RED** (Zillow model). Green washed out against green terrain (parks/grass on the roadmap, trees/lawns on satellite); red has no competition on a map. `aiMatch` stays blue.
- **Hover → the compact card preview pops up** on the map and the pin **enlarges** (desktop split-view). No recolor (already red).
- Keep the **"New" label** when under 24h, and the **▼ price-cut arrow**.
- **Framing:** the entire-metro view centers on **Kansas City (~25mi radius, no border)** — never the listing spread (leads 150mi out flung it to the lake). City views keep their boundary; the desktop split pane floors the city fit at zoom 12 so it isn't too wide.
- Source: `lib/listings/map-markers.ts` (canvas) + `components/listings/GoogleListingsMap.tsx`.

## The engagement hydration bug (still open)

Documented in [Engagement Stats](/guides/engagement-stats): social engagement can render dead / "0 until you refresh" on first visit — a React **#418** hydration mismatch when a **client** component computes time from `Date.now()` at render (server-now ≠ hydration-now). Fix pattern: compute lifecycle time **server-side, pass as a prop** (`lib/listings/lifecycle-time.ts`), never `Date.now()` in a client component/builder. Reported again 2026-08-08 on the detail page → treat as a regression or a new `Date.now()` leak.

### 2026-09-08 · lane-compact-card — #257.350: the compact card's top line, its address, and its width

**Zach walked production on his phone at 2:03pm and filed the card's layout as one ruling.** Verbatim:
*"On production, some of the houses have 2 days on HouseLoop to the right of the price, and some of
them have it under the price. On the compact card, it has to be to the right of the price."* ·
*"On mobile, the address is getting truncated."* · *"We can't even see 'house for sale' on the compact
cards."* · *"Our cards are way too tall. They need to be wider."*

- **Found — the two layouts were ONE layout.** The census that opened this lane confirmed
  `HOME_RENDER_LAW`'s premise: there is exactly one horizontal-card renderer,
  `components/listing/ListingCard.tsx`. The divergence Zach saw came from a single word on the price
  row — `flex-wrap`. Measured on prod at 390 (iPhone 14 emulation): the same markup rendered the row
  **31px tall** on the 358px search card (price, days and kebab on one line) and **63px tall** on the
  250px shelf card, where the days span wrapped and dragged the `ml-auto` kebab down with it. Nobody
  wrote a second layout; CSS wrote it whenever the content did not fit.
- **Also measured on prod, same run:** the address was truncated on **6 of 6** shelf cards (the street
  needed 222px and was given 101, because the `, City, ST` tail was `shrink-0` and ate the box first);
  "House for sale" was invisible at 390 by two mechanisms at once (a `hidden sm:inline` **viewport**
  breakpoint, and the short word that replaced it rendering at x=245 inside a body ending at x=237);
  cards were 250×399 on the phone and 295×433 on the desktop.
- **Built.** The top line is now one non-wrapping row — **price · 📅 time · ⋯ kebab** — at every
  width, with `shrink-0` on the price and the kebab so the only thing that can give is the copy,
  which gives by swapping rendition, never by truncating. The words are `4 days active` /
  `Active on 9/12` (`cardTimeLabel` in `lib/listings/card-labels.ts`); `lifecycleTimeMetric` still
  supplies the number, so it remains one fact from one deriver — **and the detail page keeps
  "43 days on HouseLoop", which Zach signed off by name.** It is a bare icon and text, never a pill.
  The address is one line that steps to 12px rather than truncating. The shelf card is **340px** on
  the phone and **360px** from `lg`.
- **The three thresholds are container queries on the card body, and each is its own measured
  number** — `@[304px]` for the time rendition, `@[348px]` for the address's type step, `@[420px]`
  for the facts row's. Keying them all to one tidy value shipped a truncated address at 358 and a
  clipped facts row at 340; the facts row's number in particular was bought by a real listing
  (`2 beds | 3.5 baths | 1,201 sqft | Townhome for sale`), because a half-bath is two characters no
  fixture had.
- **Proven.** `components/listing/card-350-pixels.test.mjs` reads pixels in a real browser at both
  `QA_VIEWPORT_MATRIX` configs — **RED against production** (24 failures naming the split row, the
  old words, 141/180 truncated addresses and the missing label), **GREEN against the fixed tree**
  (125 assertions). The overflow case Zach named himself — `$13,000,000` + `231 days active` + the
  kebab — is a real render at `/dev/card-overflow`, at every shipped width, with a sub-threshold
  control that proves the ellipsis backstop actually fires.
- **Measured after, both configs:** top row **63px → 31px**; shelf card **250×399 → 340×424** on the
  phone and **295×433 → 360×439** on the desktop (the aspect goes 1.60 → 1.25 and 1.47 → 1.22 — the
  cards are much wider and proportionally much shorter, though the 4:3 photo grows with the width);
  search card **358×448 → 358×438**; **zero** truncated addresses and **zero** split rows across 204
  cards per config.
- **Verdict:** shipped. The one rendition still narrower than this ruling governs is the Myli band's
  own scaled carousel card (~200px of layout width), which passes `compact` and is named — not
  skipped — by the ratchet.
