# AI CONTAINMENT LAW — NO UNGOVERNED AI ACTION (CONSTITUTIONAL, signed by Zach 2026-08-12)

## THE ONE RULE

**If a human cannot point to the EXACT visible playbook/canvas step that authorized an AI action, the AI is
NOT allowed to perform that action.**

The AI never "falls back" to acting. Absence of authorization is a REJECT, never a default-to-send.

## WHY THIS EXISTS

2026-08-12: the e-sign chase text **texted a real lead at ~midnight, 3 hours after Zach's own text** — an
automated AI-ish send that fired with NO visible step, while a harness was supposedly restricting all AI
sends. Zach, verbatim: *"we cannot have the AI randomly texting people… an AI absolutely cannot do anything
other than [through a visible gate]… there's an AI master switch for any type of AI communication."* The
midnight text proved that outbound paths exist that bypass the controls we THINK we have. This law closes
that class for good.

## THE CENTRAL AUTHORIZATION GATE (every AI outbound passes ONE gate)

**No AI-originated outbound communication reaches ANY provider (Twilio, Gmail, iMessage, or any other)
unless it passes through ONE central authorization gate.** Not "most workflows." Not "Myli usually." EVERY
single path. The gate is layered on the send sever (`lib/ai/authorized-send.ts`) — the single wire choke.

The gate checks, **IN THIS ORDER — a disabled PARENT switch always wins over anything below it:**

1. **Master AI enabled** (the AI master switch — off = no AI communication, anywhere, period)
2. **AI communications enabled**
3. **The specific AI employee / clone enabled** (per-service "AI team on duty"; the per-LEAD clone toggle)
4. **The channel enabled** (sms / imessage / email)
5. **An active playbook/workflow EXECUTION exists** (a real `playbook_execution_id`)
6. **The EXACT visible step exists** (a real `step_id` drawn on the canvas)
7. **That step is allowed to send**
8. **Business-hours rule passes, or delays VISIBLY** (never a hidden clamp)
9. **Recipient is not suppressed / unsubscribed** (`hl_suppressions`)

## THE FAIL-SAFE (non-negotiable — the rule that would have stopped the midnight text)

**An outbound AI message with NO `playbook_execution_id`, NO `step_id`, or that fails the gate → is
REJECTED. It NEVER falls back to sending.** No path anywhere may downgrade-and-send on a failed check.
Every AI outbound is stamped with its authorizing execution + step, or it does not leave.

## CHANNEL-AGNOSTIC — THE GATE IS BEFORE TRANSPORT; A NEW CHANNEL IS DENY-BY-DEFAULT (Zach 8/12)

Zach, verbatim: *"whether myli sends a twilio text or a blue bubble text or a smoke signal or unleashes a
homing pigeon — if i toggle her off she shouldn't have access to anything."*

The gate does NOT live per-channel. It sits at the ONE point where an AI's intent to communicate is
authorized, **BEFORE** the message is routed to any transport. Consequences, all LAW:
- **Toggling an AI off removes access to EVERY channel at once** — Twilio, BlueBubbles, Gmail/email, and
  any future transport. No per-channel exception; no per-channel patch substitutes for the choke.
- **A NEW send channel is DENY-BY-DEFAULT for AI.** It must be structurally impossible to add a transport
  an AI can reach without routing through the gate. Every transport function is reachable only through the
  authorized wire; an AI-composed send that has not passed the gate is rejected at the transport boundary,
  whatever the channel.
- **Per-channel fixes are defense-in-depth, never the primary control** (e.g. the BlueBubbles
  scheduled-send actor forward). The primary control is the single choke + the master switch above it.

## EVERY TOGGLE MUST ACTUALLY GATE

Every AI on/off control in the cockpit — **AI paused · all-AI master · per-AI-service · AI-team-on-duty ·
the per-lead AI clone toggle · Myli off** — must GATE THE SEND AT THE WIRE, not merely change the UI. A
toggle that reads "off" while a send still leaves is a P0 lie. "Cosmetic toggle" is the exact defect this
law forbids. (Audit: `.claude/reports/enterprise-qa/ai-containment.md`.)

## AN AI MESSAGE TO A TEAM MEMBER IS STILL AN AI MESSAGE (Zach 8/12 — corrects an earlier Lee ruling)

The discriminator is NOT recipient (lead vs agent). It is **composed-by-AI vs deterministic-system.**
- **AI-composed** — Myli, a clone, or ANY model decided to send it or wrote the words → it is AI
  communication and MUST pass the AI gate + be killable by the AI master switch, **REGARDLESS of who
  receives it.** Myli texting 2,000 agents a midnight *"your buyer still hasn't signed — want me to nudge
  them?"* is precisely the thing the master switch exists to stop.
- **System-deterministic** — a fixed rule fires a fixed template, no model in the loop → not AI; it
  bypasses the AI gate but is killable by the SYSTEM-notification toggle.

So `kind:'notification'` may skip the AI check **ONLY when the actor is a deterministic system/job — NEVER
when an AI composed it.** (This REVERSES Lee's earlier 8/12 "agent-facing = bypass" ruling. Zach, verbatim:
*"an ai message to a team member is still an ai message… otherwise myli could message 2,000 agents a random
midnight notification."*)

## TWO CATALOGS — AI COMMUNICATIONS vs AI SYSTEMS (Zach 8/12 PM — corrects the single-master model)

Zach: *"catalogs are two separate systems: AI Communications and AI Systems… we don't want to stop any of
the AI jobs at all: SEO, Vision, Tagging, Summaries. All that needs to be left on."* So the AI catalog
splits in two, and the "master" is a **COMMUNICATIONS** master, NOT an everything-killer:

- **AI COMMUNICATIONS** (`layer='ai_comms'`) — Myli messaging · clones · AI notifications · AI-composed
  workflow texts. Has **THE MASTER KILL-SWITCH**: off = every AI comm stops, **including in-canvas
  workflow texts** (Zach confirmed — those become playbooks later). Each function also individually
  toggleable. This is where the containment gate (Part 3) enforces.
- **AI SYSTEMS** (`layer='ai_system'`) — SEO articles · vision tagging · sold-home summaries · dossiers ·
  call analysis · AI search · content narratives. **LEFT ON.** The comms master does NOT halt these.
  Each still has its OWN toggle so Zach can kill one to debug, default ON. (Frequency throttles later —
  schema hook only, no UI now.)
- **SYSTEM notifications** (deterministic, non-AI — pagers, digests, health alerts) are NOT in the AI
  catalog at all; they have their own on/off and are never silenced by an AI toggle (silencing the pager
  when you mute the AI is how an outage goes unnoticed).

A disabled parent ALWAYS wins over a child *within its catalog*. The comms master is Zach's debug
kill-switch for OUTBOUND AI — flip it and no AI message leaves, while the Systems keep thinking/tagging/
summarizing. (This REVERSES the audit's "master over all three gate points" and Lee's earlier "master
halts all jobs" — jobs stay on by Zach's explicit ruling.)

## THE AI CONTROL CENTER (under the AI section — the enterprise surface)

A master list of EVERY AI system event/function — Myli messaging · the clone · AI notifications · SEO
articles · AI summaries · every AI job — each row showing its **RUNS** and its **COST**, with a per-function
**on/off toggle**, under a **MASTER toggle**. The toggles are NOT cosmetic: each gates the real function at
its execution point (the containment gate for sends; a job guard for SEO/summaries/etc.). This is the
single place Zach turns AI off — wholesale or per-function — to debug, and sees what each AI function is
running and costing.

## NOTHING EXTERNAL LIVES AS INVISIBLE MIDDLEWARE

The three hidden workflow behaviors are resolved as: the **e-sign chase = KILLED** until it exists as an
explicit canvas step; **standby handoff = a VISIBLE terminal step / playbook setting**; **business-hours
delay = a VISIBLE send-policy node / policy on the send step**. Nothing that communicates externally may
run as hidden middleware. (Composes with `WORKFLOW_CANVAS_IS_TRUTH` + `INNGEST_OR_IT_DOESNT_EXIST`.)

6. Switch writes; run a reconciliation period.
7. Remove DB3 credentials + code paths; DROP `hl_txn` (RULE #0 — typed Zach approval).
