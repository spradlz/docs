## THE ANATOMY OF A CARD (every card, no exceptions)
`[ ACTOR HEADSHOT ] · Actor name + specific sentence · [ kind ICON ] · SPECIFIC SUBTEXT · (house photo if a home card)`

1. **ACTOR HEADSHOT** — the real avatar of who did it:
   - Human agent (Zach Spradling, any agent) → the agent's real headshot photo.
   - **Myli (AI)** → Caleb's Apple **Pixar headshot** (`.claude/reports/homeowner-dashboard-design/myli-avatar-pixar.png`)
     WITH a small **3-star AI badge sticking out of the bottom-right of her circle** (identifies AI). Verbatim Zach.
   - Lead → initials/photo. System/job → a small system glyph.
2. **REAL KIND ICON** — the actual glyph for the action (never a grey empty circle on the card face). Secondary
   to the headshot (a badge beside the sentence / on the avatar).
3. **SPECIFIC SUBTEXT** (required on every card) — the specifics of THAT activity, e.g.
   - created an email campaign → "Spring Listings Blast · to 1,240 past clients"
   - saved a property → "7928 Halsey St, Lenexa KS · $285,000"
   - created a saved search → "Overland Park · 3bd · under $250k"
   - status change → "Pending → In Progress"
   - Myli updated her context → "about Brent Morris · noted 0–3 mo timeline + $450k budget"

## ⭐ NO TRUNCATION — ANYWHERE (Zach 8/12)
"nothing can be truncated." The saved-search name, the subtext, the address — everything shows in FULL.
No ellipsis, no cut-off. Wrap, don't truncate.

## ⭐ THE COLOR GRAMMAR (Zach 8/12 — resolves the "35 color drifts")
- **property_saved → RED**
- **property_shared → GREEN**
- **property_liked → BLUE**
- **the rest of WEBSITE activity (viewed, searched, requests, registration…) → BLUE**

- **NO all-red "created saved search"** — saved_search_created is BLUE, not red.
(⚠ brand law normally reserves green for SMS/success — Zach is explicitly signing green=shared here; his call.)

## ⭐ ZACH'S CATALOG SIGN-OFF MARKS (8/12, walked 141/188 checked) — the edits
These come from Zach walking the published catalog. Apply, rebuild, republish, he re-walks the unchecked ~47.

### 1. ⭐ STACKED CARD for EVERY task_* and appointment_* (the big structural one)
A task/appointment renders as TWO stacked cards under ONE headshot:
- TOP = a COMPACT actor line: [Zach headshot + calendar/check icon] "Zach Spradling dispositioned an
  appointment · pending → completed" (or "created an appointment" · "marked a task complete" · "created a task").
- BELOW = the FULL AppointmentCard / TaskRecordCard, with NO headshot on it (headshot lives only on the top line).
So the full object card sits UNDER what the actor did to it. ONE headshot, two cards. Zach: "the task is below
the card… you'd only have one Zach Spradling icon, two cards." Applies to ALL task_* + appointment_* kinds.
Zach did NOT check these until he sees them.

### 2. ⭐ HouseLoop icon = ROUND, everywhere
Currently a SQUARE favicon inside a circle. Make the HouseLoop/system mark a round circle. Applies to
idx_sync_completed, delivery_failed, and EVERY HouseLoop/system card. "Anything with HouseLoop = round favicon."

### 3. ACTOR + sentence fixes
- ai_toggle_enabled / ai_toggle_disabled ("turned the AI twin on/off") → actor = the HUMAN (Zach) or the
  SYSTEM, NOT Myli. "Zach Spradling turned the AI twin on/off." Myli does not toggle the twin.
- appointment-outcome kinds: "created an appointment outcome" is WRONG → "dispositioned an appointment ·
  marked pending → completed" (you dispose/mark an outcome, never "create" one). Clicking it should link to
  the actual appointment.
- ai_send_blocked "Myli was blocked from sending" → doesn't make sense; reconsider sentence/actor.
- "Myli activated the AI clone for Brent Morris" → Zach SIGNS the CONCEPT: a rule can have Myli/the system
  activate the AGENT'S AI clone when the agent doesn't answer within ~5 min ("Myli talking through the clone").
  REMOVE the "never quotes price" subtext (stupid). KEY DISTINCTION Zach drew: Myli ≠ the AI clone/twin — the
  clone for a lead is a DIFFERENT entity; Myli can ACT (activate it via the rule) but she is not the twin.

### 4. WEBSITE activity (property_viewed/saved/liked/shared/searched, home cards) → NO lead avatar circle
Remove the lead avatar on these hero/home cards — "it'll wrap everything… on the website I don't need any
Brent Morris circle icon." No actor avatar on website/home cards.

### 5. ZILLOW INSIGHT (zillow_insight_*) → the ZILLOW logo, not the HouseLoop logo.

### 6. REGISTRATION cards (registered_* / registration_completed) → structured HERO card
- Blue card, white HouseLoop logo + "HouseLoop.ai" at the bottom.
- A white circle TYPE badge top-LEFT (a registered-person-+ icon) naming the registration type.
- Structured: Name · Phone · Email · the via-phrase ("registered via direct join on HouseLoop" / "while
  requesting a tour"…). For a TOUR registration add a scheduled-tour icon + the property address.

### 7. LEAD CREATED (lead_created, e.g. Zillow Preferred Buyer) → HERO card
Structure: source · type · city · phone · email.

### 8. IN-APP MESSAGE (zillow_message_*) → keep BM avatar + the Zillow icon; REMOVE the middle
text-message/conversation icon. No 3-icon stack — just [BM avatar] "Brent Morris sent an in-app message to
Zach Spradling" + Zillow icon.

### 9. TEXT/SMS cards → KEEP the person avatar (Zach headshot / BM). Approved as-is.

STANDING: everything else Zach checked (141/188) is approved as it renders.
