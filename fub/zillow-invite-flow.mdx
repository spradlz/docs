## The one-paragraph version

An agent sends a lead a **Zillow "work together" invite** (a FUB email template). The lead clicks
their link, creates or signs into a Zillow account, and Zillow connects them to our team as their
**My Agent**. From that moment Zillow forwards their behavior to Follow Up Boss, FUB fires webhooks
at us, and the CRM mints a lead with cards and bells. Measured end-to-end on 8/19:
**consumer tour request → CRM card + bell in ~5 seconds.**

## THE STANDARDIZED INVITE LINK (Zach-ruled 8/19)

**The team uses ONE standardized invite link in every reactivation message:**

```
https://www.zillow.com/invitation/details?token=01408db7-8763-4e93-8e8d-67949b07dec9
```

- **The token is a BEARER credential: whoever clicks it, connects.** That is exactly why one
  standardized link works — proven twice on 8/19: two different consumer accounts
  (admin@bossloop.ai and titan@aititans.ai) each clicked a recycled link and each minted its own
  full chain (connection → My Agent → FUB person → CRM lead).
- FUB's own invite template also mints per-lead tokens (two real invites showed different tokens),
  but the CAMPAIGN standard is the ONE link above — paste it as a constant, never as a merge field.
- ⚠ One quirk observed: an in-app-originated mint can create a **duplicate FUB person** ("No name")
  even when a person with that email already exists — FUB does not dedup these mints.

## The connection mechanics

- **No paid program required.** My Agent connection + behavior forwarding works without Zillow
  Premier Agent advertising spend (the team account already has PA seats; the consumer-side
  connection itself is free).
- **Propagation delay is real.** After a consumer connects, FUB's "My Agent status: connected" and
  the Zillow insights panel can lag by several minutes. Do not diagnose a broken rig inside that
  window — wait, then re-read.
- **"Additional email addresses" on the Zillow profile are identity-matching only.** Never add a
  team member's own address to a consumer test account — it anchors their personal profile.

## The two roads in — and why they behave differently

| Road | Transport | Latency | Signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Behavior road** | Zillow → FUB → our webhooks | **~5 seconds** | tour/property inquiries, saves, views, searches — the whole behavior family |
| **In-app message road** | Chrome scrape of the FUB UI (no webhook exists) | rotation-dependent | Zillow in-app messages, both directions |

The behavior road is the proven fast path: on 8/19 a consumer tour request became a FUB Property
Inquiry → CRM lead mint → `tour_requested` card with the full house payload → bell in ~5s, and
Myli's context carried 5 facts from it.

### ⚠ The in-app message road's 3-defect stack (found 8/19, live-proven)

A test consumer's first in-app messages reached **no tape, no card, no bell**. Autopsy:

1. **Enrollment gap** — in-app messages only arrive via the Chrome scrape, and a **brand-new person
   is not in the scrape rotation**, so their messages are invisible until something else enrolls
   them. A new lead's first message is the strongest signal in the pipeline and it goes silent.
2. **Direction inversion** — when force-scraped, the tape stamped the LEAD's own messages as
   outgoing (`is_incoming = false`). Task #180's disease class, proven on the inbound arm.
3. **Refusal cascade** — the card writer refuses "outgoing" texts by design (#188), so a
   wrong-direction row never cards and never bells even after capture.

**Cure chain (top of the #159 pipeline overhaul):** mint-time enrollment (webhook mints a person →
immediate targeted scrape) → DOM-arm direction fix → inbound files → card + bell at behavior-road
parity. Fixes go in the road, never hand-filed rows.

## HOW-TO: The Zillow Reactivation Play (the manual)

The play that turns a cold 49k-book contact into a connected, tracked Zillow relationship. Steps 1–2
run today (Zach sends manually; templates are copy only). Steps marked FUTURE are AI-composed sends
and ship only behind the AI containment gate (visible playbook step, killable by the comms master,
under the per-human send budget).

**Step 1 — Myli makes contact first.** Myli (the client care coordinator) engages the lead —
answers questions, builds context, logs everything to the activity log. No invite yet. The invite
always lands AFTER a real conversation exists, so it reads as follow-through, not spam.

**Step 2 — The founder's personal invite.** A personal (never branded) email/text from the team
founder: *"Hey {{contact_first_name}} — you've been talking with my client coordinator Myli, and I
wanted to reach out personally and invite you to work together on Zillow…"* + THE STANDARDIZED LINK
(above). Agent name is a merge field. Today Zach sends this manually; FUTURE = his clone sends it as
a visible campaign step. Myli never sends the invite — the founder does.

**Step 3 — The message body is assembled from blocks** (Zach's AI context blocks invention, 8/19):

| Block type | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **CANNED block** | Fixed literal copy — the offer, the signature, compliance | "I personally oversee our team on every home…" |
| **MERGE field** | Dumb token | `{{contact_first_name}}`, `{{agent_first_name}}` |
| **THE LINK** | The standardized invite link, pasted as a constant | the URL above |
| **AI CONTEXT BLOCK** | A stored PROMPT for one section — at send time the composer receives the prompt + the lead's REAL context (activity log via the one reader, Myli conversation history, saved searches, household memory) and writes just that section | *"Reference the most relevant recent thing this person did or told Myli — the home they toured, the neighborhood they asked about. Never invent facts; if no history exists, fall back gracefully."* |

Block prompts are DATA on the template row, never code — a human can always point at the words'
authorization. This kills the canned-campaign smell at scale: each of 49k emails references the real
home the person toured, while the skeleton (offer, link, compliance) stays deterministic.

**Step 4 — The lead clicks the link.** Signs into (or creates) their Zillow account → Zillow
connects them to the team as their My Agent. Bearer semantics: whoever clicks, connects.

**Step 5 — Wait out the propagation delay.** FUB's "My Agent: connected" and the insights panel can
lag by minutes after the click. Zillow-side timestamps can lag too. Do not diagnose a broken rig
inside that window.

**Step 6 — The behavior road goes live.** From connection forward, tours/saves/views/searches flow
Zillow → FUB webhooks → CRM: lead minted, cards with the house payload, bell in ~5 seconds, Myli's
context fed. In-app MESSAGES ride the slower scrape road (see the 3-defect section above — its cure
is the top of the #159 overhaul).

**Step 7 — Myli continues the relationship** with full context: the connection, the browsing
behavior, and the conversation history all on one lead record.
