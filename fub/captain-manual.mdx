## The queue — one, honest

`fub.rehydration_queue` on the capture store, claimed with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`.
**`priority='high'` means "a recent real-time trigger" and nothing else** — the 47,866-row legacy
backlog was re-baselined to `low` on 8/22 (two rounds, reversal CSVs in
`~/hl-reversals/queue-rebaseline-0822/`). A writer that stamps `high` on non-real-time work is
recreating the fake-priority disease. The detector logs `lane=fast` / `lane=BACKLOG` on every
enqueue so starvation can never hide again. (The fold into `fub.job_queue` is a signed follow-on.)

The detector's cursor is **two facts, never one**: `last_inapp_at` (what we SAW — always advances)
and `settled_at` (the message was DEALT WITH — written only when WE enqueued it or an exists-check
proves it on the tape). Unsettled rows are re-checked every poll outside the recency walk; rows
older than the 24h repair window are HELD and reported loudly, never auto-fired (a months-old gap
is a desk backfill decision).

## Fixture law (Zach, 8/22, after the carrier ate three "delivered" messages)

- **Fixture accounts carry REALISTIC names.** "Jake Smith-test" passes carriers; "Boss Test Lead
  205" was silently eaten on three Twilio-`delivered` sends.
- **Message bodies are normal human sentences.** No nonces, no codes, no "test/QA/proof" language —
  the carrier reads the body, and its spam ML filters what looks synthetic *after* reporting
  delivered. **Markers live in timestamps and metadata, never in carrier-read text.**
- **A send is proven only by a fresh page load or the counterparty surface** (FUB's
  `lastInboxAppMessage` advancing). A cleared compose box is an optimistic echo, not a send.
- ⛔ Never fire at a live lead. Test identities + owned Twilio lines only.

## Deploy — cattle, not pets

Canonical source: `tools/fub-scraper/` in the repo. Deploy = scp + **md5 verified on both sides** +
`pm2 restart <app> --update-env` + `pm2 save`, via the deploy script (G6 lane's receipt names it).
A process not in the repo, not in the ecosystem config, or without a heartbeat **does not exist** —
the in-app sweep sat unscheduled for months with everyone believing it ran; that class of lie is
what this rule kills. CRM-side functions are Inngest: registered in `serve()`, PUT-synced after
deploy, and **proven only by a run in `hl_run_log`** — an event id or `modified:true` proves
nothing.

## Health — DOWN only on proof

- **The canary** (hourly :17) is the webhook road's proof: `arrived:true` = the road works;
  `injected:false` with a reason = an honest refusal to investigate (that is how the missing prod
  env vars were caught on its very first run).
- **Heartbeats testify about the process that writes them**, nothing more. A heartbeat with a
  non-draining queue is a live process failing at its job — read both.
- **Silence is AMBER-with-facts, never DOWN.** DOWN requires a failed active proof or a dead writer
  assertion.
- **`synced_at` on tape rows does NOT date tape arrival** — it is the capture-store stamp carried
  verbatim. Latency claims come from direct observation (run-log, polling for existence), never
  from that column.
- Instrument warning: `.env` carries `FUB_TAPE_DATABASE_URL` as an ALIAS of
  `FUB_RAILWAY_DATABASE_URL` (same database) while `FUB_CAPTURE_DATABASE_URL` is Camp David.
  "Diffing the tape against the mirror" with the two Railway names reads one store twice. The G6
  receipt owns the rename.
