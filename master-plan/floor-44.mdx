- [ ] The bookmark icon dies repo-wide — heart means saved everywhere — One idea, one symbol: a heart means saved. The dashboard, the emails and the saved-homes screen all stop using a bookmark for the identical action. <sub>*One concept one symbol; sweep dashboard/emails/saved-homes.*</sub>
