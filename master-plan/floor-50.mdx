Now there is one function and one answer. <sub>*LANDED 8/28, desk-verified 17/17 (the ~18:55 desk log in .claude/reports/myli-os/ZACH-REVIEW-RULINGS-2026-08-27.md). lib/dashboard/comm-counts.ts feeds the card, the drill-down and Myli’s team answers.*</sub> `FRI 8/28`
- [x] Stop counting our own test leads in the headline numbers — A hundred and fifty of the messages on that card were our own rehearsals talking to themselves. <sub>*LANDED 8/28: texts 3,418 → 3,274 and calls 364 → 356; the 144 and 8 that left were proven to the row to be our own harness traffic.*</sub> `FRI 8/28`
- [x] Say on the card exactly what each number counts — The number was never wrong — it just never said it meant this year, both directions, everybody’s leads. Undeclared is worse than wrong. <sub>*LANDED 8/28: the caption reads "2026 · sent + received · all teams · excl. test leads".*</sub> `FRI 8/28`
- [x] Decide whether agent-to-agent texts belong in the headline number or on their own line — A thousand of the messages on that card were our own team texting each other, not clients. Zach ruled it: the headline counts only what we sent out to leads, and internal traffic stays out of it. <sub>*ZACH RULED 2026-08-28 ~18:10: the dashboard headline number is ONLY outbound attempts to LEADS — outbound texts + outbound calls to leads. Agent-to-agent/internal traffic is EXCLUDED from the headline (not a second line on the card). Implementation row added.*</sub> `FRI 8/28`
- [ ] Fill in the team on leads so the numbers can be scoped by team instead of labelled all teams — Eighty-three percent of leads carry no team today, so scoping by team right now would silently drop most of the record while looking more precise. <sub>*Ruling B from the ~18:55 desk log: team-scope deferred honestly with the label saying "all teams"; the team backfill is its own item.*</sub>
- [ ] Put eyes on the new caption on the live site at the next deploy — The number is proven against the record. Nobody has yet looked at the card with their own eyes on the real site.
- [ ] Make the dashboard headline count only outbound texts and calls to leads, excluding all team-to-team traffic — Zach 8/28: "our dashboard headline number is only outbound attempts to leads, or outbound text or outbound calls to leads." The 1,015 internal texts leave the headline.

### The bar, and her answer to a filter question — 0/6

- [ ] Widen the header search into one long bar with an Ask Myli button inside it — The search box is capped narrow today. It becomes the full width of the header with her headshot sitting in it.
- [ ] Put her headshot on the mobile header and open a full-width ask sheet when you tap it — On the phone there is no filter estate at all, so she is the filter.
- [ ] Make the mobile lead list read the same filtered query the desktop reads — Today the phone pulls a couple of hundred rows and sifts them on the device, which is why the counts and the desktop disagree.
- [ ] Turn a typed question into a filtered lead list, with the filters shown as chips you can remove — Her answer is the same filtered list the buttons produce, so she never becomes a second way of asking the record the same question.
- [ ] Send a question that looks like a name, an email or a phone straight to the search, never to the model — The common case stays instant and costs nothing, and it cannot be mis-read as a request to do something.
- [ ] Prove a typed question and the equivalent filter buttons produce exactly the same list — If the two ever disagree, one of them is lying and nobody would know which.

### The answers, the actions, and the microphone — 0/12

- [ ] Answer a question about a number from the one function that owns it, and say what the number counts — She says "you set 14 appointments in August, that is 22% of the 63 new leads assigned to you" — never a bare percentage.
- [ ] Say plainly which numbers she cannot answer yet, and shrink that list only by building the function — A number leaves the cannot-answer list by gaining a real function behind it, never by her learning to guess.
- [ ] Let her draft a text to one person from the bar, held for your approval, never sent on its own — The bar is a new mouth, not a new permission. Everything it does still stops at the desk for your yes.
- [ ] Say out loud that she can only message one person until the seats land — A bar that silently drops the second recipient is worse than one that says it cannot do that yet.
- [ ] Let her send a document from the bar through the same approval road — Sending a showing agreement was one of Zach’s own examples, and the tool for it already describes itself.
- [ ] Add microphone dictation so you can talk to the bar instead of typing — Zach asked for it to work the way dictation works in a chat app, including in the installed phone app. <sub>*Signed 8/28 late evening: native browser speech recognition, graceful absence where it is unsupported.*</sub>
- [ ] Grow what the bar can do by describing more of her tools, never by adding special cases to the bar — Everything running through her is not a bigger bar — it is every tool describing itself, which the toolbox floor is already doing.
- [ ] Give Myli real metric logic over the structured record: any counted thing, narrowed by any time window, in one composable tool — Zach 8/28: "not with hardcoded filters but with logic that looks through structured data." One metrics tool composes what × when × who — never a canned filter per question.
- [ ] Answer how many appointments we set this month, keeping appointments SET and appointments HELD as two different questions — Set = when it was booked; held = when it happened. She answers either, and says which one she's counting.
- [ ] Answer how much commission the team has earned this month from the commission record — Zach's named question. Reads the canonical commission tables; says the window and the scope out loud.
- [ ] Answer this-month-versus-last-month comparisons, like our appointment set rate, computed fresh from the record both times — Zach 8/28: "what is our appt set rate this month compared to last month, that type of shit." Rates are computed from two live aggregates, never stored.
- [ ] Audit that every metric Zach names has a structured home with a real date column, and fix the record where it does not — Zach 8/28: "our data better be that damn structured that it would be easy as shit to calculate in about 2 seconds." A metric she can't compute is a data-shape bug, not a Myli limitation.
