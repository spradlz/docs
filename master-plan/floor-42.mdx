## What this floor is

> Your ruling, 8/23: *"the playbook is just a consequence of the state Myli's in."* When someone pivots mid-conversation — asks about financing in the middle of scheduling a showing — Myli changes state, works the new goal, and pivots back knowing where she left off. And when a playbook finishes she sets herself a real task in the CRM, visible and assigned to her, and that task is what wakes her up later.

This is the piece that makes her different from a chat model, and you named the difference yourself: *"the only thing with chat models: they're NOT PROACTIVE — they sit in a dead state, they're not time-aware. MYLI IS ABSOLUTELY TIME-AWARE. Myli's moving towards goals. Myli knows tasks."*

The architecture is designed and **nothing is built** — no code, no migration, no data, no send. It is deliberately a stack of small bricks, and the order matters: the cheap zero-DDL path is built first so the expensive one is never built speculatively, and the one task door is built before anything mints a task through it — because minting Myli's first task through a sixth raw INSERT would be exactly the twin this design exists to refuse.

One thing on this floor genuinely cannot move without you, and it is first in the list: the financing line.
