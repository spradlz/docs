**The insight that makes 7 days possible:** 39% of the backlog needs verification, not construction
— and verification parallelizes almost perfectly. The marginal cost of completeness is near zero
when the machine walks, screenshots, and files the receipt itself.

## The four engines (the novel part)

### 1 · THE VERIFY FLEET — burns the 160 first (easiest wins first, made mechanical)
A standing fleet of walk-lanes pulls verify-only items straight off the registry (the proof bits
ARE the queue — no new tracking system). Each item: walked in real Chrome per its own evidence
note → ten-field audit → registry bit flips → the master plan regenerates → a storybook link lands.
An item that FAILS its walk is an honest red: it moves to the build pile with the failure named —
that is the sweep doing its job, not a delay. Pace: ~55/day → the pile is gone by Day 3.

### 2 · THE DAILY DECISION DIGEST — 44 Zach items stop blocking lanes
Every evening Zach gets ONE page: each open decision as a yes/no with the evidence laid out and a
recommended default (the triangulation doc is the template — 31 questions became zero). He rules
10–15 a night in minutes. A decision nobody surfaced properly is a lane blocked for days; a
decision in the digest is 30 seconds.

### 3 · THE BUILD CONVEYOR — 192 builds in dependency order
The registry gains three fields (`needs`, `effort` S/M/L, `beta` flag) and a generated **QUEUE
page**: topologically sorted, beta-critical path always on top, easiest-first inside each tier.
Rules of the conveyor:
- **UI items pass the WIREFRAME GATE**: no locked HTML → it enters the *wireframe factory* first
  (drafts produced for Zach's nightly lock — his sign is the only serial step), then builds as a
  verbatim port. Never prose-built again (the 8/16 tours-band lesson).
- **Every project's PRD gap closes as its first item** — the "no PRD on file" banners on the
  project pages are themselves queue entries.
- Waves of typed lanes (build / port / data), receipts, two trains a day, gauntlet ×2 on anything
  client-facing. Pace: ~30–35/day.

### 4 · THE TWO NAMED CRITICAL PATHS — priority lanes that never wait in the general queue
**BETA-50:** C&J gauntlet green ×2 → state-engine MVP (the state table + Myli's login behavior +
search-bar awareness) → household summary (#81) → the 50 leads' staging derivations (history, AI
summary, facts → drafted searches) → provisioning wave in batches of 10 (Zach approves each
search, then texts the intro himself) → the clone cadence drawn as visible playbook steps behind
the containment gate. **Win metric: appointments set.**
**PAST-CLIENT POUR:** the FUB staging table + jobs (the ledger-315 PRD) → digest #1 carries Zach's
three calls (Linville thaw grant · the additive-only resolver fill nod · spouse-record closures) →
**THE POUR** (all ~1,700, honest accounting) → home-value cards + the #69 two-screen dashboard for
every past client. **Win metric: every past client has a live home-value surface.**

## What could break this, stated up front
- **Zach's serial steps** (wireframe locks, the nightly digest, the pour grants) — the plan
  batches them into evenings so lanes never idle on him, but skipped digests stall Day 4+.
- **Honest reds in the verify sweep** — some of the 160 will fail their walk; the plan absorbs
  ~20% failures into the build waves without moving the end date.
- **The 192 hides folds and duplicates** — inspection will kill some outright (marked as records,
  never fake-proven) and reveal a few icebergs; icebergs get named the day they surface, with the
  scope change on this page, not discovered on Day 7.
- **Platform outages** (today's 529 wave) — the recovery loop pattern is standing.
