## What we are actually doing

> Your own workflow made into the product. On the phone it looks like your iPhone: a thread per lead, one standing group thread for the team, and Myli sitting in it — listening, extracting the facts, updating the lead and setting the next follow-up so nobody types into a CRM. Every teammate gets a user detail page that IS a lead detail page in another mode: their appointments and tasks on the left, their whole conversation inbox in the middle, Myli on the right. Zach's cut of the MVP: Trish does not even need a phone number — she texts through the app and Myli is in the thread. The seat model underneath means the thread, the lead record and all three user profiles read the same rows, so they cannot disagree about who was in a conversation.
