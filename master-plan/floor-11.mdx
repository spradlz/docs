## What this floor is

> Retiring the old trigger-events table so the activity catalog is the single vocabulary — the way Bedrock signed it. Four phases, in dependency order, and the order changed once already when a phase turned out to depend on another.

Phase 1 poured the facts additively — 49 wiring statuses, 16 clean activity words, 30 automation flags — every count guarded by an assertion, so a wrong count writes nothing. Zero catalog values were overwritten.

The lesson it bought is worth keeping: a structural test that says 'the target word is NULL' is **necessary and never sufficient**, because a word poured onto an umbrella kind becomes a claim about every member underneath it.

Phase 2 shipped partially and neither foreign key was promoted. It also uncovered a live defect: two settings surfaces selected registration events by the SHAPE of their name rather than by the catalog, so the website integration page had been showing one of eight real registration events. That exact bug had been diagnosed and fixed once before, on a sibling count, and never swept for its twins.
