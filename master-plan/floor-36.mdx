## What this floor is

> Scenarios run against the live engine with fake people and real rails: distress handled with empathy and no escalation, legal questions handed off, an honest answer when she is asked if she is a bot, silence after a STOP. It is how the flagship gets proven before it ever touches a real client.

Slice 1 went green on 8/23 — seven of seven scenarios against the real engine, zero stray sends — and it earned its keep immediately by catching three real walls nobody had measured: the intent dedup key blocked multi-turn conversation, the send budget was scoped to one test line, and the touch budget was not enforced at all.

The multi-turn wall is now gone. The key gained an **occasion** — a cause rather than a counter — so a reply keys on the inbound it answers and a nudge keys on the stretch of silence it breaks. A real three-turn conversation runs, a second reply to the same inbound still refuses as a duplicate, and a ghoster's second nudge still refuses because the touch budget is exhausted.
