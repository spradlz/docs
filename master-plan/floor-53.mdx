- [ ] Put a hardcore character budget on every block, enforced at save, not suggested — Zach: "we need to freaking be hardcore on the character budget." Over budget refuses to save, with the count in your face. `MON 8/31`
- [ ] Move the six code-buried instruction fragments into the governed blocks, one at a time, each proven unchanged in composition — Her real instructions live in six code files today; each moves into the editable store with a proof the assembled prompt stayed byte-identical before any wording changes. `SUN 9/6`
- [ ] Put the fifty-thousand-character persona on a diet into the named blocks, through the desk — Six times past where the field says stop. The diet is editorial work with the desk signing what survives, what moves to the knowledge base, and what dies. `SUN 9/6`

### The knowledge base — what she knows is not who she is — 0/2

- [ ] Split the brokerage knowledge out of the persona into a knowledge base she can grab — Zach's diagnosis: the clone persona is a knowledge base crammed into a persona. Who-she-is stays small; what-she-knows becomes lookup material. `MON 8/31`
- [ ] Let a knowledge entry be edited on its own page with its own history, never inside a prompt blob — Fixing the commission-split answer is a knowledge edit, not brain surgery. `SUN 9/6`

### Releases, the bench cage, and rollback — 0/3

- [ ] Make publishing a release: an immutable snapshot of the whole composed persona, with a pointer you move — Desk decision under Zach's delegation: rules keep their per-rule history, and publishing freezes the composed whole; rolling back is moving the pointer, one motion. `MON 8/31`
- [ ] Cage the bench gate: no release publishes until it passes a simulated conversation against the test personas — Zach: "it has to pass a simulated conversation first… cage it, same law as the flow-walk receipt." The simulator is the flow judge pointed at the draft release. `SUN 9/6`
- [ ] Prove the cage red first: a deliberately broken draft must fail the bench and be refused publication — An instrument nobody has seen fail is an instrument nobody should believe. `SUN 9/6`

### The dials, and the laws that become checks — 0/3

- [ ] Build the dials: warmer, less chatty, and their siblings as configuration that adjusts her style block — Zach: "turning dials… as opposed to going in and hard coding." Each dial writes a governed style value; no prose surgery for a tone change. `SUN 9/6`
- [ ] Audit her always-on voice laws and convert the mechanizable ones into typed checks — A prose rule may simply not fire — the field admits it. The laws that can be machine-checked become checks; taste stays prose; the desk adjudicates each. `FRI 9/4`
- [ ] Point her phone voice settings at real structured fields and retire the hand-typed chips — Her voice id, greeting and speed become settings that the phone system actually reads — the display-only chips die. `FRI 9/4`

### The programming surface — 0/2
