## What this floor is

> There are three parsers behind what looks like one search, and on 8/23 they disagreed with each other on nine of fifteen real queries. The hardening is done. What remains is the taxonomy for places smaller than a city, and the console itself — the screen that would have made every one of those defects visible instead of requiring a lane to go find them.

The census was blunt: the search bar answered 9 of 15 correctly, the AI modal 12 of 15, and **Myli's own search arm 0 of 15** — it was posting the wrong body shape and getting a 400 on every single call. The school filter **failed open**, so an unresolvable school put all 7,645 metro homes under a heading that said 'your school zone'. County was unspeakable from language on both roads. And your own signed price-hedge band was running on only one of the two roads.

Six gates closed the same day, every one red-first with the numbers on the record: the AI arm went 4-of-34 to 34-of-34 · 'five acres in Johnson County' went from 659 results to the true 81 · a school query went from 311 to the true 75 · the fail-open filter now fails closed, 18 of 18 · the two roads now parse identically, 16 of 16 · and the single-story fix went from 11 red to 35 green.

⚠ The single-story fix is code-complete and **its backfill has not run**, so 214 homes — 7.4% of single-story inventory — are still invisible to a single-story search. Code that is right and data that is stale is still a wrong answer on the screen.

⭐ **THE V12 REDESIGN (2026-08-24) — the console Zach will actually test.** He ruled he will not re-test the current console, so V12 is the only road to a signature. His dictation, near-verbatim: *"I love the middle-column horizontal cards, but there's no way to see those results on the map and still have the cards. I want a split with a single scroll of homes plus the map, remove the left and right column — a hybrid of creating a new saved search and seeing an existing search, where I can go through and click to add houses. Maybe that IS what create-saved-search needs to look like: edit, select, see — all in one place. And filters should be right at the middle column to edit the search on the fly."*

**THE INTERVIEWED SPEC, seven points.** **(1)** ONE workspace for create AND view/edit — the only difference between them is whether a search is loaded. **(2)** The LEFT RAIL (lead context) STAYS, collapsible to a thin 64px rail — never a full-screen mode change; THE RIGHT COLUMN IS REMOVED, and that removal is what frees the width. **(3)** TWO view modes: **CARDS** — one middle column of the horizontal cards with the full filter bar above them, so filters are editable without ever opening the map · **MAP** — map left, a single card column right, filters still visible. **(4)** A card click opens the FULL LISTING as an overlay; closing returns to the exact scroll position. **(5)** PICK-FIRST, CHOOSE-AFTER — ＋ on cards builds the picked set, and then one control asks the destination: put them on the client's dashboard list, or send. **(6)** Save on a loaded search raises the ask-every-time dialog (update their standing search vs save as new); the verify screen stays as the save confirm. **(7)** Entry: clicking an existing saved search opens the workspace already loaded with it.

**Round-2 amendments (same day):** the Tours section moves into the left rail · entry simplified to a direct load, dropping the map-preview-that-expands step · the card column caps at **1,020px** · the picked-set button's words are locked as **"Use these 3 homes ▾"** — it cannot be *Send homes*, because one of the two destinations is not a send, and it must not be *Save to a list*, which names the plumbing instead of the intent. ⚠ **ONE QUESTION IS STILL OPEN AT LOCK TIME, and it is Zach's own sentence:** *"I might be wrong about the left rail always staying present."* The rail is drawn as staying — in both states — and pressing Map does NOT collapse it for you, because a layout that dissolves its own rails is a different layout wearing a costume. That is the item to settle at the lock. **The v11 LOCKED wireframe remains the record of the CURRENT build; v12 supersedes nothing until Zach says the HTML is locked.**
