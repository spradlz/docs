## What we are actually doing

> One inbox that never lies: texts, group texts, calls, and email through one transport (deliver()), filed on every person they touched via the seat model — a group message to Conner and Jasmeet lands on BOTH records with the real sender named. Group text v2 rides Twilio's group-MMS primitive with OUR OWN membership binding (never the provider's), gated by the no-go test before any live send. The group-text transport question was settled live on 8/23: a Twilio-owned number measurably cannot sit in a Twilio group, while a real shared iMessage group forms and reaches an app-less member — so the production shape is the iMessage road with ONE thread estate over two transports.

## Where it is at

The group thread is REAL and it is dangerous, both proven the same night. On 2026-08-23 at ~23:11 Myli spoke into a group from her own line, the app-less Twilio line received it, and Zach's reply came back - a genuine shared group, with iMessage/BlueBubbles confirmed as the production shape (a Twilio-owned number measurably cannot sit in a Twilio group).

⛔ And the same test measured the danger model: on send #1 every signal we have read GREEN - ok:true, participants(2), error:0, service "iMessage" - and the member received nothing. A group that forms perfectly and silently excludes a non-iMessage member is why a PER-MEMBER DELIVERY ASSERTION is now a non-negotiable of the capture build, not a nice-to-have.

⭐ 2026-08-25 — THE REHEARSAL ROAD IS CLOSED, MEASURED, AND THAT IS THE FINDING. builder-groupsms built nothing on purpose: three of its four brief items were already shipped, and the fourth — proving group fan-out on our own test lines — is structurally impossible. All three owned numbers return Twilio 400 / code 50407 on the address-only bind. Verbatim from the lane: "the property that removed the privacy trap is the same property that removes our ability to rehearse it." So the per-member delivery assertion, the seat resolution at capture, the echo-path fixes and the fail-CLOSED allowlist all stay on the build list — but the PROOF of a real group now has exactly one road, and it runs through Zach's handset.
