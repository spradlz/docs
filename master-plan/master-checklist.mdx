> **HOW TO READ A ROW — the number and the tag are two DIFFERENT things:**
>
> - **№ (1–196)** is the BUILD ORDER, top to bottom. One list, never renumbered.
> - **The bracket tag names the item's HOME BOARD** — where its receipts live. It is **never a floor number**: **[FUB-n]** = item n on the [FUB Pipeline board](https://crm.houseloop.ai/boards/fub-pipeline) · **[BED-n]** = the [Bedrock checklist](https://crm.houseloop.ai/boards/bedrock-checklist) · **[SELL-n]** = the [Seller Engine board](https://crm.houseloop.ai/boards/seller-engine) · **[TASK-n]** = the task board. So **[FUB-8]** reads "FUB board, item 8" — it has nothing to do with Floor 8.
> - **Floors appear ONLY in the section headers** (WEEK 1 · Floor 1 …) and match [THE BLUEPRINT](/master-plan) floor-for-floor. Each week also has its own chapter page in the left nav.
>
> Floor filing is the desk's v1 triage — placements refine as change-orders (filed inward), never appends.
