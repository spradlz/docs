> **Zach's own metaphor, 2026-08-23:** he is the general contractor, and this is **the master
> blueprint set** — it reads like a book in construction order, foundation → subfloor → floor one
> → up, and *"the foundation plan has all the specs for each thing on the foundation."*

**There are two artifacts, and they are deliberately separate.**

| | **📘 THE BLUEPRINT SET** *(this page and its chapters)* | **🗓 THE SCHEDULE** *([the punch list](/master-plan/punch-list))* |
|---|---|---|
| what it answers | **what the thing IS, and how it is built** | **what we are doing today, and the atomic items to do it** |
| time | **timeless.** A blueprint page does not care what day it is | **dated.** Monday's page is Monday's |
| shape | one page = the complete spec for one thing, plus its signed drawings | day → the day's things → each thing's floor → its checkboxes |
| you open it when | you are asking *"how does this work / what did we decide"* | you are asking *"what are we doing right now"* |

**The bridge between them is one link, in one direction:** an item on a day page links **into** its
blueprint page. Nothing else crosses. Schedule never gets smeared into a spec, and a spec never
starts carrying dates — that smear is exactly how a plan and a design drift apart until neither is
trusted.

## How to read a blueprint page

Every chapter has the same three parts, in the same order:

1. **What we are actually doing** — plain English, no jargon, no acronyms you have to look up.
2. **The signed drawings** — the locked wireframes, rendered on the page. Where a drawing is locked
   and has no rendered image, the page **says so** rather than quietly omitting it. A board that
   names a wireframe it cannot show is a gap, and it is filed as one.
3. **The checklist** — every item with its four proof bits: **BUILT** (the code exists) · **WIRED**
   (actors, catalog, tables) · **WALKED** (somebody used it through the UI) · **MYLI** (the same
   action is callable by her, proven). A tick needs all of them, twice through the gauntlet.
