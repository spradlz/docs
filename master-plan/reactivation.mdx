## What we are actually doing

> Pointing Myli at an asset no startup has: **49,000 real relationships** with years of Zillow
> browsing history, **1,779 past clients** whose homes we can value every month, and a licensed team

The unit of the play is not a campaign, it is **a person landing on a dashboard where Myli is
already mid-conversation with them.** The reactivation happens on a surface we own, in a thread

a mail-merge.

## The ramp — never all at once

The cohort law is not a pacing preference; the ramp **is** the control that catches what nobody
named in advance.

| # | cohort | when | what it proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **PROVE** — the 100-lead cohort | now → week 4 | Myli handles real people end to end on the proven roads. Every scenario rehearsed in the simulator first — the cheapest place in the company to be wrong. |
| 2 | **THE WARM BOOK** — the 1,779 past clients | weeks 3–8 | Home-value dashboards provisioned, the monthly value pulse, Myli's check-ins. These people already love us: the lowest-risk, highest-trust reactivation and the referral engine. |
| 3 | **THE BROWSING-SEEDED RAMP** — cohorts from the 30K | weeks 6–12 | Personalized from what we already know: *"You were looking at 4-beds in Olathe last spring — want to see what they're going for now?"* Email and text through the gates, each cohort measured before the next widens. |
| 4 | **THE FULL BOOK** — the remaining ~19K | months 3–6 | The my-agent invites and the newsletter as the standing heartbeat. |

## The funnel honesty

3–4 closings a month by month 6 means roughly 8–12 active transactions, which at conservative
reactivation numbers means **~2,000–3,000 people genuinely active on dashboards by month 4–5** —
which the ramp above is sized to produce. 8–10 a month by months 9–12 is the same funnel at
full-book scale plus the referral flywheel.

**We hold priors, not data.** Reactivation response rate is a named known-unknown, and the first
cohorts exist to price it. If we run behind the levers are: widen cohorts faster, deepen the
newsletter, and lean on the past-client book, which converts at multiples of cold reactivation.

## The rails every send rides

- **[The AI containment gate](/command-center/ai-containment-law)** — no AI-composed message reaches
  any provider without passing the master switch, the channel, and a visible drawn step. Absence of
  authorization is a REJECT, never a default-to-send.
- **A human at every ramp gate.** Zach reads transcripts and approves each widening; the copy desk
  reads every new surface's first output.
- **Deliverability is warmed by the ramp itself** — a 49K book's sending reputation is the reason
  cohort 4 is months out and not weeks.
