## What this floor is

> Your own workflow, made into the product: you, Ana and Trish work out of a group text; Myli sits in that thread, extracts the facts, updates the lead and sets the next follow-up. Every teammate gets a user detail page that looks exactly like a lead detail page — their appointments and tasks on the left, their whole conversation inbox in the middle, Myli on the right.

Three bands, and they are ordered by dependency, not by appetite. **Band A is the drawings — and they are LOCKED as of 2026-08-24**, Zach verbatim: *"yes the mobile app group thread drawing is locked."* The port now builds from `docs/wireframes/mobile-groupthread-LOCKED-2026-08-24.html` node-by-node, because the wireframe is the truth and a build from prose is how the TC bands went wrong in July. **Band B is the foundation** — seats, the in-app channel, the user page, Myli in the room. **Band C is the group-text capture** — making the real iMessage group we proved on 8/23 file correctly into the CRM.

The architecture underneath was already signed and is already partly built: the seat model (one fact, a seat per person it touched, so the thread, the lead record and every user profile read the same rows), the /m shell, the partner-compose road, and AI containment — which means the in-app channel arrives deny-by-default for AI and reaches a person only through the one gate.

What this floor newly creates: the **user-profile estate**, the **Negotiator role** (your wife writes the offers — that role does not exist today), the **in-app message channel**, and **Myli DM threads** per user.
