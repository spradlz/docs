## What we are actually doing

> Myli as a governed teammate: every AI outbound passes ONE authorization gate (master switch → channel → visible playbook step) and an unauthorized send is REJECTED, never defaulted. deliver() is the single transport for all 8 provider primitives — a new channel is deny-by-default. AI Systems (SEO, tagging, summaries) stay on their own switches so killing comms never kills the thinking.
