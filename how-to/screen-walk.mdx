<Info>
**Why this exists:** during the 7/28 outage, every audit was green while the product was broken five
ways. The label drift, the purple-hand icon, the missing HouseLoop section, the silent tour requests —
**every single one was found by Zach looking at a screen**, none by a query, a test, or an audit.
Audits check tables; eyes check the product; the product is the truth. This makes the eyes a formal,
repeatable step. **A gate does not close on numbers alone.**
</Info>

## The rule

At every gate close (and after any change touching the catalog, the feeds, or notifications), walk the
list below **on the live site** (never localhost), on **desktop AND mobile**, and record what you saw —
one line per row, "matches" or the defect. A row you didn't look at is a row you didn't verify.

## The walk

### Lead detail (pick one message-heavy lead + one call-heavy lead)
| Look at | It promises |
|---|---|
| The tab row + counts | All · AI · Inquiries · Messages · Calls · Emails · Notes · Appointments · Tasks · Website · Deals · Starred · FUB — every count clicks through to exactly that many rows |
| **All** | Every row from every other tab. All = the union, zero exceptions (including Myli memory, deals, stage moves) |
| **Messages** | Bodies render (never blank cards) · correct icon (text bubble, never the purple hand) · Zillow in-app rows carry the via-Zillow chip + home card · from → to provenance line |
| **Calls** | Real call cards: number, duration, direction, recording/summary where present |
| **Website** + pills | Views / Saves / Shares / Likes / Searches counts match what the pill claims when clicked |
| Badges + icons | Every row has a real badge — an unknown/fallback icon means a kind lost its catalog row |

### Trigger Events
| Look at | It promises |
|---|---|
| The HouseLoop.ai band | Present, one folder per source, every door listed |
| The Follow Up Boss band | Loads fully (never stuck on "Loading…"), toggles reflect the database |
| Any toggle you know you set | Shows the state you set it to |

### Notifications (settings + your phone)
| Look at | It promises |
|---|---|
| /settings/notifications | Your toggles as you set them; SENT counts move when things send |
| Your phone | The classes you toggled to TEXT actually text your cell — check against the SENT drill-down |

### Run Log
| Look at | It promises |
|---|---|
| The tabs | Rows flowing; a `dispatch-drop` row names its reason + classKey (drops are never invisible) |
| Inngest functions | No function silently absent that should be running (compare with the dashboard) |

## Recording

Paste the walk result into the working artifact at the gate boundary: date, surfaces walked, defects
found (or "clean"). A defect found here is a finding, not a failure — the whole point is that the screen
catches what the audits can't.
