## 📁 Listing Documents

**Everything that puts a house on the market.** Folder label: `Listing Documents` (categories `listing`,
`seller_disclosure`).

| Document | Status | Signed by | Send when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Listing Agreement** | category live · **no template imported** | seller · co-seller · listing agent · broker | Before the house goes on the market |
| **Seller's Disclosure** | category live · **no template imported** | seller · co-seller | With the listing agreement |
| **Lead-Based Paint Disclosure** | **not built** — no category, no template | seller · co-seller · buyer | Any home built before 1978 |

<Note>
**Lead-Based Paint is federal, and it is the one with a real timing rule** — the buyer gets a 10-day
opportunity to inspect, and the form is signed by BOTH sides at different moments in the deal. When it is
imported it earns a full section here (and a cross-reference from the buyer playbook), because it is a
document a person can get wrong. Most forms will not need that.
</Note>

## The rules that apply to every seller document

These are the same laws as the buyer side — they are properties of the signing engine, not of a side.

**The packet is the unit, not the form.** A document with attachments merges into ONE PDF and ONE signing
session.

**Unchecked means off the document.** A party unchecked in the send modal is not a signer, is not on the
share list, and receives nothing.

**We add nothing to the signed PDF.** Documenso's sealed output is archived byte-for-byte, including the
signing certificate it appends as the last page.

**A missing date line is not a missing date field.** Where a form prints no "(Date)" caption, none is
placed — the extractor never invents a coordinate (`SIGNATURE_ANCHORING.md`).

**Run the gate before a template ships.** `scripts/audit-field-maps.ts` checks every form: page count
against the real PDF, a signature box for every signing party, and no stacked boxes.
