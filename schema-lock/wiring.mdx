## The 148-kind wiring map

**Measured 2026-08-03: WIRED 40 · DATA-ONLY 14 · UNWIRED 94.**

- **WIRED** — a code writer names the kind literal.
- **DATA-ONLY** — rows arrive but no literal writer: these flow through DYNAMIC writers (the pixel lane, the
  FUB lane, `logMessage` channel mapping) that a literal-grep cannot see. Several were live-certified during
  the audit (`contact_requested`, `saved_search_alert_sent`, `sms_received` ×861) — treat DATA-ONLY as alive.
- **UNWIRED** — zero writers AND zero rows ever: signed vocabulary nothing produces. This is the load-bearing
  number. The 94 split into (a) the audited-invisible actions above — Batch 1 of the fix plan wires them —
  and (b) pre-built vocabulary for unshipped surfaces (campaigns, merge fields, voicemail, loan status),
  ruled wire-or-wait at the consolidation review.
