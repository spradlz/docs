Settings → Notifications controls every alert that goes to **you** (or the team). Client-facing messages
(tour confirmations, welcome emails, listing alerts) are **not here** — those are workflows managed per
trigger event in [Registration Trigger Events](/settings/lead-platform).

## The two panels

| Panel | Who it's for | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| **How I get notified** | Each agent, personally | Where your alerts land, which events ping you |
| **Sent from** | Admins only | The outbound email + SMS identity for system alerts |

---

## How I get notified

### My Channels

The phone numbers and email addresses where YOUR alerts land. Add a phone → you get text pings. Add an
email → you get email pings. You can have multiple of each. Each recipient controls their own channels;
no agent can configure another agent's.

### Event toggles (BELL / TEXT / EMAIL)

Every notification event has three independent delivery toggles:

| Toggle | What it does |
|---|---|
| **BELL** | In-app bell (always records regardless of TEXT/EMAIL) |
| **TEXT** | Sends an SMS to each number in My Channels |
| **EMAIL** | Sends an email to each address in My Channels |

**TIMING** shows either "Instant" (fires immediately) or an "Nh" delay chip (Agent Nudges only — fires
after N hours of silence). **SENT** is a live count of deliveries; click it to see the drill-down log of
who got what and when. **VIEW** (eye) opens the notification template. **CONFIG** (gear) opens the
per-event timing rule.

#### The Connected Phone rule

**Text / Missed Call / Voicemail are always locked ON for SMS.** A lead reaching you always texts your
cell — the TEXT toggle for these three events is disabled (you can't turn it off). This matches how
`ALWAYS_SMS_CLASSES` works in the dispatch layer (`dispatch.ts`).

### Event sections

#### New Lead Activity

One class: **New Lead** (`lead_created`). Fires on any first-time registration from any source
(HouseLoop, Zillow, Realtor.com, etc.). The message body shows what the lead did — saved a search,
requested a tour, shared a home — plus their CRM link.

#### Existing Lead Activity

Per-activity classes for leads already in the CRM. Grouped by channel:

| Sub-group | Events |
|---|---|
| **Website activity** | Home liked · Home saved · Home shared · Saved search · Tour request · Contact request · Seller request · Home value report · Property viewed |
| **Calls** | Call received · Missed call · Voicemail |
| **Text** | New message (always SMS, locked ON) |
| **Emails** | Email received |

The Calls / Text / Emails sub-groups were folded into Existing Lead Activity on **2026-07-14** (Zach) —
the old standalone "Communications" section is gone. Each event toggles independently.

#### Lead Lifecycle

Stage changed · Lead assigned · Added as collaborator · AI clone activated · Lead reassigned ·
Appointment scheduled · Appointment completed.

#### Agent Nudges ⏰

Time-based reminders that fire when a human drops the ball:

| Nudge | Fires when |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledge Handoff** | Myli hands the lead to you and you haven't introduced yourself within the configured delay |
| **Response Needed** | A lead messaged you (text / email / call) and you haven't replied within the configured delay |

Each nudge has a **timing rule** (CONFIG gear) — threshold hours + on/off per channel. The default is
Instant. If AI Failover is on, the clone steps in when Acknowledge Handoff fires.

#### AI Follow-Up 🤖

Pings when the AI does something: AI Phone Calls · AI Messages Sent · AI Facts Captured · AI Homes
Searched · AI Stages Advanced · AI Appointments Set. Off by default — opt in to the ones you want.

#### Website Activity 🌐

**Daily website digest** — a morning email summarizing the last 24h on houseloop.ai (real humans,
registrations, homes viewed, searches, requests). **Bot/crawler breakdown** — include the crawlers &
bots section in the digest.

#### Transaction Deal Notifications 📁

Loop created · Under contract · Document signed by client · Milestone reached · Transaction closed ·
Transaction lost · Status changed · TC assigned · Key date approaching.

Fires to the deal's assigned agent and the TC coordinator.

#### System Alerts

Live health badges for: Gmail disconnected · BlueBubbles disconnected · Phone / Twilio disconnected.

#### Inbox Notifications

Subscribe to any inbox (an agent's personal inbox or a shared line) to get pinged on every text, call,
and email that hits it — even for leads not assigned to you. This is the "watch my wife's inbox"
control. Stacks with the notification scope below.

## Admin-only controls

### Global delivery master switches

Account-wide kill switches that sit **on top of** everyone's personal prefs:

- **Email off** → no notification email sends to anyone on the team, regardless of their per-event
  setting. The in-app bell still records.
- **SMS off** → no notification text sends to anyone.

Use these to silence a channel account-wide in one flip. Only visible to admins (`canEdit = true`).

### Which leads notify me (scope)

Admins can choose:

| Scope | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Only leads assigned to me** | Default — you hear about a lead when it's yours |
| **All leads — any agent** | Every registration + activity across the whole team |

Regular agents always see only their assigned leads; the scope card is hidden for non-admins.

## Sent from

Two outbound identities for **internal / team alerts**:

| Sender | Key | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Email sender** | `team_alerts` | All system + team email notifications |
| **SMS sender (INTERNAL)** | `outbound_sms` | All system + team text notifications (agent alerts) |

These are **not** the addresses leads see — see [Brand line (public)](#brand-line-public) below.

### Brand line (public)

A dedicated public identity the **HouseLoop brand** uses when texting or emailing a lead:

| Sender | Key | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand SMS line** | `brand_public_sms` | Listing alerts, saved-search digests, registration texts |
| **Brand email sender** | `brand_public_email` | Same — every branded lead email sends from this one address |

Promote a number from the pool in [Phone Provider](/settings/phone-provider). Keep this separate from
the internal `outbound_sms` alert line so leads don't see your internal (913) 800-4470 number.

## Architecture notes (for builders)

- **`createNotification`** (`lib/notifications/create.ts`) fans out to the lead owner + every
  `notif_scope='all'` admin. The 5-min dedup keys on `(entity_type, entity_id)` — each distinct
  inbound signal fires its own notification; reprocesses are dropped.
- **`dispatch.ts`** gates SMS on `ALWAYS_SMS_CLASSES` (new_message, missed_call, voicemail) regardless
  of pref. Email/SMS prefs are per-agent per-event; the global master sits above them.
- **Inbound Twilio SMS → notification:** the SMS webhook (`app/api/phone/webhooks/sms/route.ts`)
  inserts into `hl_bb_events` (channel='sms') and calls `notifyInboundMessage` with the row's UUID `id`
  (NOT the raw `SM…` MessageSid — that string can't cast to the `uuid` column in `hl_notification`).
- **Inbound iMessage → notification:** same path via the BB webhook
  (`app/api/bb/inbound/route.ts`); BlueBubbles GUIDs are UUID-formatted so they pass directly.
- **Notification scope** persisted in `hl_users.notif_scope` ('assigned' | 'all'). Admins can change
  it; non-admins are always clamped to 'assigned' server-side.
- **Per-event timing** (Agent Nudges delay) stored in `hl_agent_nudge_rules` and read by the Inngest
  nudge function at fire time.
- **Sent counts** come from `/api/notification-sends` (run-log source='notification'). Click the count
  → drill-down modal with who + when.
