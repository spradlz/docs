**In the app:** CRM sidebar → **User Settings** → **Profile Settings** — [`/settings/profile`](https://crm.houseloop.ai/settings/profile)

<Info>There is no standalone profile form. `/settings/profile` immediately resolves the signed-in user and redirects to that user's row in the shared member editor at `/settings/team/agents/[id]` — the same page used to edit any teammate. "My Profile" / "Account" and "edit a member" are one editor, self or others. The old dedicated profile form has been retired.</Info>

## What it does

`/settings/profile` is **not a standalone form** — it is a thin redirect. On load it calls `GET /api/profile` to find the signed-in user's id, then `router.replace`s to that user's row in the shared member editor at **`/settings/team/agents/{id}`**. If the id can't be resolved it falls back to `/settings/team`. While resolving it shows an "Opening your profile…" placeholder.

The member editor is one scrollable page with a filter bar — `All · Permissions · Profile · Connections · Roles` — plus an `AI Clone` tab for human members. Edits auto-save (debounced ~600ms after the last change) and a **Save** button forces an immediate save.

## What you configure

The identity fields live in the member editor's **Profile** section:

| Field | Editable | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Name | Yes | `full_name`. Re-issues the session cookie so the sidebar name updates immediately. |
| Email | No | Read-only sign-in identity, managed via Google. |
| Phone | Yes | `phone`. |
| Time zone | Yes | `timezone` (Eastern / Central / Mountain / Pacific). |

The surrounding editor also exposes (subject to permissions), documented in full with the Team / member editor:

- **Contact Cards** — the member's **vCard** (savable contact) and **Digital Business Card** (branded image). See below.
- **Permissions** — Access level (Owner / Admin / Member) and member Type (Human / AI Agent). Only admins can change another member's access, and you cannot change your **own** access.
- **Connections** — Email, Calendar, Phone, and iMessage channel connections. The Email connection is the
  Gmail integration — **[documented in full below](#connections--the-gmail-integration)**, including what it
  does and does not sync today.
- **Roles** — job roles for humans; AI-kind roles with per-role persona/tool/doc overrides for AI agents.
- **AI Clone** — human members only: assign and configure the AI clone that sends as you (your phone, email, calendar).

## Contact Cards (vCard + Digital Business Card)

Each human member (and Myli, the standalone AI agent) has **two** shareable cards, edited in two sections of the member editor. Both are **data-driven** — every field lives in `hl_users.data`, nothing is hardcoded — and both are **public** so a text message can attach them (Twilio MMS / iMessage fetch them unauthenticated, the same mechanism Follow Up Boss uses).

<Info>These are two different things. The **vCard** is a real savable contact (`.vcf`) — the recipient taps it and gets the native "Add Contact" screen. The **Digital Business Card** is a branded **image** (`.png`) you text as a picture — no save button. Per-human **AI clones** never get cards; standalone agents (Myli) do.</Info>

### vCard

The savable contact. Editable fields: **Name** (`full_name`), **Title** (`data.title`), **Mobile** (`data.card_phone` — a dropdown of the member's own assigned lines: Twilio dialer / iMessage / cell), **Email** (`data.card_email` — a dropdown of known addresses or a custom one), **Homepage** (`data.card_homepage`, default `houseloop.ai`), and **Notes** (`data.card_note`, default `Spradling Group powered by HouseLoop`).

The section shows a live preview of what the card looks like when sent — the **collapsed** suggestion row and the **expanded** iOS contact card — plus a Copy-link and Download-`.vcf` control.

Card construction rules (learned on real devices):

- The **header is name + photo only** — no `ORG`, no company mash-up. iOS combines title + company into the line above the name and it reads cluttered, so the brand lives in **Notes** instead. If **both** title and company are blank, iOS shows "Unknown" there, so a title (e.g. "Realtor") is what fills that slot.
- The **headshot is embedded as base64** inside the `.vcf`. iOS refuses to fetch a remote `PHOTO;VALUE=URI`, so a linked photo shows initials — the bytes must be inline.
- Served as `text/vcard` at **`/api/team/vcard/[id]`** (public). A shareable web version lives at **`/c/[id]`** (a clean contact page with a "Save to Contacts" button).

### Digital Business Card

A branded card **image** you text as a picture. Editable fields: **Name**, **Title**, **Mobile**, **Email** (same data as the vCard — an edit syncs both). Rendered server-side (Satori / `next/og`) as a PNG at **`/api/team/card-image/[id]`** (public). The preview in the editor **is** that exact PNG shown large, so it always matches what gets sent.

### Sending them

The lead text composer gets two separate controls — **Send vCard** and **Send Business Card** — that attach the respective artifact (SMS via Twilio `MediaUrl`, iMessage via a BlueBubbles attachment). The picker list comes from **`/api/team/cards`**.

<Tip>A member's headshot lives in the website app at `houseloop.ai/brand/headshots/<name>.jpg` and must be `https` or the card falls back to initials. `tools/houseloop-cards/enhance-headshot.mjs` (Gemini "nano-banana") can face-restore/upscale a rough photo into a clean headshot while preserving the person's identity.</Tip>

## Data source

- **Route page:** `apps/houseloop-crm/app/(crm)/settings/profile/page.tsx` — client component, `GET /api/profile` → redirect.
- **`/api/profile`** (`GET` / `PATCH`) — `apps/houseloop-crm/app/api/profile/route.ts`. Reads/writes the current user's row in **`hl_users`**: `id`, `full_name`, `email`, `role`, `phone`, `timezone`, `avatar_url`, and `data->>'notification_email'`. `PATCH` updates `full_name`, `phone`, `timezone`, and `data.notification_email`, then re-issues the session cookie. (The redirect page itself only calls `GET`; edits made in the member editor go through `PATCH /api/team/users/{id}`.)
- **`/api/team/users/[id]`** — the member editor's primary load/save. Joins **`hl_users`** with role assignments (**`hl_member_role`**) and connection state: **`hl_oauth_connections`**, **`hl_dialer_assignments`**, **`hl_imessage_assignments`**, **`hl_calendar_connections`**. Editor at `apps/houseloop-crm/app/(crm)/settings/team/agents/[id]/page.tsx`.
- **`/api/team/roles`** — the role catalog (human and AI-kind roles).
- **`/api/me`** — the viewer's identity + access level, used to gate the Access dropdown.
- **`/api/team/users/[id]/roles/[roleId]`** (`PATCH`) — persists per-role overrides (persona/tools/docs/workflow_ids) on **`hl_member_role`**.

**Contact Cards:**

- **`/api/team/vcard/[id]`** (`GET`, public) — serves the savable `.vcf` (`text/vcard`), headshot embedded as base64. Built by `apps/houseloop-crm/lib/vcard.ts` (`buildVcard` / `vcardForUser`).
- **`/api/team/card-image/[id]`** (`GET`, public) — the Digital Business Card PNG (Satori / `next/og`), `apps/houseloop-crm/app/api/team/card-image/[id]/route.tsx`.
- **`/api/team/card/[id]`** (`GET`) — the card's display fields as JSON (drives the in-editor previews).
- **`/api/team/cards`** (`GET`) — the card-able roster (humans + Myli; clones excluded) for the composer picker.
- **`/c/[id]`** — the public web contact page (headshot + "Save to Contacts"). Renderers: `components/team/ContactCardPreview.tsx` and `components/team/VcardSentPreview.tsx`.
- The editable card fields are stored on `hl_users.data`: `title`, `card_phone`, `card_email`, `card_note`, `card_homepage`. `PATCH /api/team/users/{id}` accepts each. All three public routes are allowlisted in `middleware.ts`.

No Inngest functions are involved; all writes are synchronous CRM API calls.

## How it fits

Profile Settings is the User Settings entry point into HouseLoop's single member model: every teammate — human or AI agent — is one row edited through one page, and "your profile" is just your own row in it. Because your profile is your own member record, the same editor governs access levels, comms connections, and AI-clone configuration, keeping signing-in identity (name/phone/time zone, Google-managed email), permissions, and channels in one place rather than a separate account screen.

## Connections — the Gmail integration

**In the app:** the member editor → **Connections** → **Email**. This is the "connected email" control, the
HouseLoop equivalent of Follow Up Boss's Connected Email.

### What being "connected" actually means

Connecting a mailbox stores a Google OAuth row in **`hl_oauth_connections`** (`provider='google'`,
`is_active=true`) holding the access + refresh token and the `gmail.readonly` scope. Two capabilities hang off
that one connection:

| Capability | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Sending as you** | A 1:1 composer email goes out through **your real Gmail** (`sendViaGmail`), so it lands in your Sent folder and threads normally. See [Email Settings](/guides/email-settings). |
| **Receiving into the CRM** | Gmail has no webhook, so we use `users.watch()` → a GCP Pub/Sub topic → push to `/api/email/webhook`. **Watches expire after 7 days**, so an Inngest cron re-arms them daily. That keep-alive is mandated by Gmail — it is *not* polling for mail. |

**Multi-account:** every connected mailbox gets its own watch, and each keeps its **own** history cursor
(`watch_history_id` + `watch_expiry` on its row) — no shared cursor, no cross-account bleed. Live today:
`zach@`, `ana@`, `myli@houseloop.ai`.

**To verify a connection is genuinely live** — "Connected" in the UI only proves a token exists:
```sql
SELECT account_email, is_active, watch_expiry, (watch_expiry > now()) AS watch_live
  FROM hl_oauth_connections WHERE provider = 'google' AND recruiter_id IS NULL;
```
`watch_live = false` means mail is **not** flowing in, however green the screen looks.

<Warning>
**⚠️ WHAT THIS INTEGRATION DOES NOT DO YET — the honest gap (audited 2026-07-30).**

Zach's expectation, and the correct one: *"FUB has an email section with connected email, which is basically
what we have, and it should sync all emails between a lead and the user in HouseLoop and pull them in."*
**It does not do that today.** Capture works; **attribution and content do not.**

**The case that proved it.** On 2026-07-29 21:06 the lender emailed a pre-approval letter:

```
From:    jnelson@fountainmortgage.com
To:      Trish.redenbaugh@gmail.com, zach@houseloop.ai,
         bradleybengel@gmail.com, Ana@houseloop.ai
Subject: Bradley Bengel Pre-Approval Letter
```

The watch **caught it** — it is in `hl_inbound_emails`. But `lead_id` is **NULL**, so it never appeared on
Bradley Bengel's lead, and FUB's own copy is `[CONTENT HIDDEN]` (a FUB user-privacy setting the scraper
**cannot** recover — waiting on FUB for this content waits forever). Five distinct defects, all in
`lib/email/gmail-realtime.ts`:

| # | Defect | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inbound matches the **sender only** — `leadIdForEmail(fromEmail)` | The lender is not a lead, so nothing matched. **Bradley's address is right there in `To` and is never checked.** This is exactly the "I'm cc'd on an email to a lead" case FUB handles. |
| 2 | `addr()` parses **one** address | A multi-recipient `To` line is passed whole to the lookup and matches nothing. |
| 3 | **`Cc` is never requested** — `metadataHeaders=From,To,Subject` | Anyone CC'd is invisible to matching. |
| 4 | Fetch is **`format=metadata`** | Headers only. `hl_inbound_emails.body_text` exists but is **always empty**; you get Gmail's ~100-char `snippet`. |
| 5 | **No attachments, at all** | No fetch, no storage, no table. The pre-approval PDF is simply not captured. |

**Plus a duplicate:** the same email produced **two** rows — one per receiving mailbox. Each mailbox assigns
its own `gmail_message_id`, and the dedupe key *is* `gmail_message_id`, so it cannot collapse them. Deduping
on the RFC `Message-ID` header (stable across mailboxes) is the fix.

**What "first-class email" requires** — the shape of the work, in dependency order:
1. Match the lead across **From + To + Cc**, every address, not just one.
2. Fetch `format=full`; persist real `body_text` (and HTML), not a snippet.
3. Fetch and store **attachments**, so a pre-approval letter is on the lead.
4. Dedupe on RFC `Message-ID`, not Gmail's per-mailbox id.
5. Suppress the FUB `[CONTENT HIDDEN]` twin once we hold the native copy, so one email is one row.

Until 1–3 land, an email between a teammate and a lead is captured but **orphaned, bodyless, and without its
attachment**. Treat "Connected" as *"we can send as you, and we are recording inbound mail"* — **not** as
"your correspondence with a lead is on the lead."
</Warning>
