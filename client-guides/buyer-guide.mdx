## What it is

The deliverable a buyer gets at (or right after) the consult. Eleven US-Letter pages, print-ready. It
answers one question — *how do we actually win the house* — and it exists because Ana asked
*"where is our buyers strategy?"* and the honest answer was that it lived in her head and in phone calls.

## The eleven pages

The structure is Zach's, given verbatim: *"what's the process, on one page, then how to win, then what's
next, the reviews, and reviews on every page spread throughout."*

| Page | Its one job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cover |
| 2 | **The process** — six steps, one sheet, reads straight down. Step 4 is flagged as the one that decides everything |
| 3 | **The highest offer does not win** — five offers on a $400,000 house where $415,000 loses to $409,000 |
| 4 | Stronger offer: close faster, and the inspection |
| 5 | Stronger offer: escalation, and earnest money |
| 6 | Stronger offer: appraisal gap, and closing costs |
| 7 | Which offer is yours — Careful / Balanced / Aggressive |
| 8 | What it costs to buy |
| 9 | What's next — three steps, and the lender |
| 10 | Reviews |
| 11 | Back cover |

A real, attributed buyer review is anchored at the foot of **every** page, with the Google mark and five
stars. That was Zach's own fix for pages that read thin, and it is a content rule, not decoration.

### Two deliberate choices

- **No numbered "levers."** An earlier cut labelled the offer terms "Lever 1 of seven" and Zach killed it:
  *"what are these random phases and levers dude."* The terms are named, never numbered.
- **The lender is named.** John Nelson, Fountain Mortgage, 913-608-7029 — with the specific ask to
  **fully underwrite**, not merely pre-approve. That single request is what makes a 10-day close possible.

## How Ana's seven map to the guide

Ana's source lists **seven** ways to strengthen an offer. The guide renders **six** named terms, because
items 6 and 7 are two directions of the same dial and share one section.

| Ana's source | In the guide |
|---|---|
| 1 — Closing Date | Close faster |
| 2 — Inspection Options | Handle the inspection deliberately |
| 3 — Escalation Clause | Beat the next offer automatically |
| 4 — Earnest Money Deposit | Put real money at risk |
| 5 — Appraisal Gap Coverage | Take the appraisal risk off the table |
| 6 — Closing Costs and Prepaid Expenses | The seller reads net, not price |
| 7 — Seller's Closing Costs | The seller reads net, not price |

<Warning>
**Open question for Ana.** Nothing was dropped — both 6 and 7 are covered in full — but the guide commits

merge is accepted. **Ana rules on this**, and this page gets updated with her answer.
</Warning>

## Sharing it

Two walls, both real:

1. **This site requires sign-in.** Fine for the team; a client or a reviewer without an account cannot open
   anything here.
2. **Mintlify will not serve the PDF at all** (see above), so it cannot be handed out from the docs even to
   someone who is signed in.

The artifact link is shareable but starts private and has to be shared deliberately from its own share menu.

**The unblock for both** is hosting the PDF on the website as a public `houseloop.ai` URL. That single step
makes it openable by Ana, by a buyer, and by any future CRM email that wants to attach or link it. It needs
Zach's go-ahead because it is a website deploy.
