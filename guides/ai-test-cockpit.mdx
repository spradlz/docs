> from the earlier section map — it is its own canonical doc/initiative)."*

This is that document. It is the canonical home for everything about the cockpit; the deep design
lives in its PRD and this page is the developer-facing index over it.

**The boundary that makes it a separate document — ruling #34:**

> **REVIEW QUEUE ≠ TEST COCKPIT.** The review queue is the LIVE surface — *"I actually review it on
> a lead,"* runs on real and test leads. **It is NOT the editing/testing place.**

So: [**Myli's Desk (the review queue)**](/guides/myli-os) is where a human approves what Myli is
about to say to a real person. **The cockpit is where you find out what she would do before anyone
is on the other end.** Two surfaces, two jobs, two documents.

## The one sentence

**Zach picks a person for Myli to talk to, picks what starts the conversation, presses go, and
watches her whole mind work in real time — and when she says something wrong, he corrects her in
place, changes the step that produced it, and runs it again.**

Almost everything under that sentence already exists. **The build is a windshield over a built
engine, plus one genuinely new capability: TEACH MODE THAT PERSISTS.**

## The signed requirement (Addendum 3, 2026-08-23)

> *"Otherwise there's an infinite amount of stuff that can happen — we need to see what Myli would
> actually do."*

1. **AI-vs-AI scenario runs.** An AI plays the PERSON — typing, replying, going quiet, raising
   curveballs — against **Myli's REAL engine**: the real state assembler, the real playbook run, the
   real gate chain, the real composer. Never a mock brain; the point is to see what she *would
   actually do*.
2. **On a TEST lead.** The scenario runs against a test identity, so every move the engine wants to
   make is exercised for real inside the standing test-leads-only gate — **the containment that
   governs live never bends for the simulator.**
3. **Time-compressed.** The simulator compresses the clock so a two-week arc plays out in minutes —
   otherwise a scenario that includes "she waits until the nudge clock expires" takes a real day to
   watch.
4. **Myli's full thinking visible in real time.** Every turn shows **which playbook** is governing ·
   **the move** she chose (or held) · **the BECAUSE** (the state facts and gate verdict that drove
   it) · **what she is waiting for** (the held moves and their clocks).

**Why it is signed law and not a tooling nice-to-have:** an infinite space of conversations cannot be
walked by hand. The simulator is how a playbook's behaviour is SEEN before a real person ever
receives a word. It composes with the harness — **the harness proves the gates refuse; the cockpit
shows what she does when the gates allow.**

## The vision, widened — ruling #35 (2026-08-27)

> *"A VISUAL testing ground for everything that today runs as backend TypeScript."*

Four things the addendum did not have:

1. **Choose the AVATAR.** You pick the persona on the other side — **and Myli has no clue who she is
   being tested against.** That blindness is the design: a scenario where the engine knows it is a
   test proves nothing about the engine.
2. **Set up the scenario, select the TRIGGER, watch it run.** The trigger is the real automation
   trigger, not a special test door. **Ruling #41** specifies the selector: a dropdown with SECTIONS
   mirroring the trigger catalog (Property Inquiries · High Intent · HouseLoop), where you pick the
   trigger *and* the LEAD SOURCE, hit enter, and the cockpit runs **the related PLAYBOOKS and the
   WORKFLOWS both**, per the enrollment mapping.
3. **TWO MODES — final shape, ruling #49.** **AUTO MODE:** it just runs, you watch it as-is.
   **TEACH MODE:** it **stops at every step**, one step at a time, exactly like the review queue's
   rhythm — approve · or *"tell Myli what to say instead"* · or add a new playbook step. Each stop
   shows the review-queue-style panel: **why** she is about to do what she is doing, what she will do
   **next**, what **facts** she captured.
4. **FAST-FORWARD speed modes** — 1 hour = 1 second, 1 day = 1 or 10 seconds. The existing time
   compression, made visual.

**Teach Mode's loop, simplified by ruling #48** (this supersedes any dry/live split — there are not
two teach variants): at any turn, *"what should Myli say instead"* → Zach **types the line** → the
scenario **plays on with his line in place** → at the end the cockpit asks *"this changed — update
the prompt, or run again?"* And **ruling #43** fixes the order: **see the results first, then update
the prompt.** Corrections target the playbook's prompt, applied after the run is reviewed.

**Scope note, ruling #45:** group text is **not** the cockpit's v1 focus. The cockpit is for
playbooks and workflows driven by **automation triggers**; group-text scenarios come later.

## What is already built — and it is more than the ruling assumes

Four facts that set the scope. Every one is a `file:line` you can open.

**① The page already ships.** "Test Cockpit" is in the sidebar (`Sidebar.tsx:66`) and carries **12
components / 3,273 lines** behind one blue tab bar with three mode tabs
(`components/settings/test-cockpit/TestCockpitShell.tsx:17-20`):

| tab | component | lines |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger Events & Workflows | `TestCockpit.tsx` | 558 |
| AI Voice | `VoiceCockpit.tsx` | 579 |
| Lead Follow-Up | `LeadFollowupCockpit.tsx` + `FollowUpMission.tsx` | 330 + 398 |

Plus the newer instrumentation: `TurnTraceBoard.tsx` and `ScenarioTraceBoard.tsx` (the per-turn
decision trail), `DirectorRail.tsx` (the Director's packet rendered live), and
`PromptEditorModal.tsx`. **Zach's vision is a FOURTH MODE in that frame, not a new page.**

**② The simulator is BUILT and more capable than the ruling assumes.**
`lib/playbooks/simulator/` is roughly **5,300 lines**: **25 live scenarios + 2 pending**, a two-sided
AI-vs-AI runner driving Myli's real engine (`runner.ts:863`), a two-road clock (`clock.ts`), a
1,383-line verdict grader and a judge model. **It cannot be fired from a screen** — today it runs
only as `RIG_CONFIRM=yes … tsx run-scenarios.manual.ts`. That is the gap: the engine exists, the
windshield does not.

**③ The Conversation Director is built and wired into the production reply path** — see
[THE MYLI OS §3](/guides/myli-os). **Ruling #40 ratified it ON for the test band**, and the cockpit's
A/B is what measures her with and without steering; the world flips on that evidence.

**④ A no-send roleplay seam already exists** — `app/api/test/lead-followup-roleplay/route.ts`. Its
header, verbatim:

> *"you type as the lead, Myli composes a reply against the lead's REAL memory/context, and NOTHING
> is sent or written to the lead. Runs the exact production brain via composeAiMessage (send/mutate
> tools stripped — it returns the draft), so preview == what she'd send. … **HARD no-send /
> no-mutate** … This is the 'real leads are production' law — a test can't pollute a lead."*

### ⚠ The one instrument pointing the wrong way

The scenario trace reader merges four lanes and **does not read `hl_tool_fire` at all** (verified:
`grep -c hl_tool_fire trace-read.ts` → **0**). Zach asked to see *"the TOOLS being called"* by name,
and the tool-call witness is exactly the table it does not read. The runner already knows why
(`runner.ts:682-686`):

> *"THE WITNESS IS `hl_tool_fire`, NEVER `hl_activity_log` … The activity log DEDUPES repeat lookups
> by design … so a transcript built from it under-reports: 8 real lookups of one address render as 1
> frozen row, and **'she never looked' gets manufactured by the instrument**."*

**That is a vacuous-green generator sitting inside the instrument that is supposed to prove
behaviour**, and it is the first thing the build fixes.

## ⛔ What the legacy Test Harness doc was, and why it was retired

**This is Zach's 8/27 complaint, and it was verified true.** The only Test Cockpit documentation in
the estate was `legacy/guides/test-harness` — titled *"fire trigger events and workflows for real,"*
carrying a `LEGACY — UNVERIFIED` banner, last touched **8/11**, and still nav-live. It mentioned
**workflow 18 times and playbook 5 times**, because it predates the entire playbook estate.

**The complaint is structural, not editorial.** The three shipped tabs above are not stale
documentation of a good product — they are an accurate description of a product built *before* the
thing it now needs to test. The doc was right about the cockpit; the cockpit is the part that is
behind. `legacy/guides/test-harness` now redirects here.

## Where the deep design lives

**The PRD is the source of record:** `.claude/reports/test-cockpit/PRD.md` (v2, 2026-08-27) — the
flow forward and backward, the Director's honest live status, the component-by-component gap table,
the screen map and the two teach roads, the AI-containment rung analysis, **the STRUCTURE_GATE
interview for Teach Mode's storage**, the ranked open decisions, and the four build slices each with
its red-first gate.

**The build slices, in order:** ① the windshield (read-only) → ② the run road (autonomous) →
③ Teach Mode + the Director A/B → ④ the clock and the trigger.

**The persona cast, the real inboxes and the phone lines it runs against are not repeated here** —
they live in [The Test Town](/command-center/test-town), which stays the fixture registry. Link it;
never copy it.

**Standing law this build inherits:** every scenario runs test-band only, inside the same
[AI Containment](/command-center/ai-containment-law) gate that governs live — the containment never
bends for the simulator, and the cockpit **must never mint a grant that reaches a real person.**
