## Calls — "Recents" (`/m/calls`)

Deliberately the iPhone Phone app (Zach locked 7/25). The rule that shaped it, verbatim:
*"when I click that number it will call it… it calls through my Twilio line that they called."*
Screen 3 of the locked wireframe below.

## Leads — the simple list (`/m/leads`)

Zach 7/25: *"our leads page is much more complex than it needs to be… what I need to know is
the lead"* — so the mobile list is name-first and minimal. Screen 4. `/m/leads/new` adds a
person with the same minimalism.

## Lead — Summary · All · Website (`/m/leads/[id]`)

Three tabs: a summary, the full All thread, and website activity — screens 5–7 of the
wireframe, per the same directive.

## Comms, Activity, Trash

- **`/m/comms`** — the conversation firehose (texts · calls · emails, person-to-person),
  newest first, live via the run-log SSE stream; tap any row → the lead.
- **`/m/activity`** — the real-time run-log of everything with filter chips (All · AI · Texts ·
  Calls · Emails · Notes · Stages · Website); event-driven, zero polling.
- **`/m/trash`** — Recently Trashed: everything a swipe threw away, newest first, restorable
  (reached from ☰ → Recently Trashed).
