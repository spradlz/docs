## 1 — One Myli: the spine and the three rooms

*SIGNED #256.34 · .34a · .36 (9/03)*

The god-tier plan for the website chat, the dashboard chat, and the home-value chat — which is to say: the plan that admits they were never three things. There is ONE person named Myli, ONE conversation with each human, and three rooms it happens in.

**The one idea:** each surface used to be its own chatbot — its own script, its own history, its own amnesia. That is WHY she lost to a ChatGPT with no data: ChatGPT is one continuous mind; she was three interns who don't share notes. The fix is not three rebuilds. It is ONE SPINE — one conversation store, one memory, one agent loop — and three MOUNTS that differ only in where the human is standing.

> **The prime directive (Zach, verbatim, the seal on this whole book):** "No hardcoding Myli. Just giving her TOOLS and the PILL SELECTION RULES that she has to use." The spine gives her tools, the state table gives her the pill rules, the voice line gives her the voice — and every sentence, every pick, every judgment is HERS. Anywhere this could be built with a template instead of a tool grant, the tool grant wins. A hardcoded Myli sentence found anywhere in the three rooms is a defect by definition.

### The spine — build once, everything rides it

| Organ | The law |
|---|---|
| **One thread store** | A CONVERSATION is a first-class object with an id, a title she writes, a surface-of-birth, and turns. ALL three rooms read and write the same store. The website's per-visit search-conversation silo and the dashboard's separate store merge onto it (adapters first, one store as the landing). |
| **One agent loop** | The loop (the state prompt + the tools + the state table) is the ONLY speaker in every room. No surface ever composes a sentence itself. Scripted openers, questionnaires, and count-dumps are deleted on sight. |
| **One memory** | Facts flow through the one extraction pipeline from EVERY room into the lead's chart; the agent reads the chart in every room (§2). Say "two kids and a golden retriever" in the modal — the home-value chat knows Biscuit. |
| **Session semantics** (#256.33a) | Chat history = a list of CONVERSATIONS (her titles, not search queries), reachable in every room. New chat = a genuinely new thread: Myli knows the PERSON (memory) but knows the conversation is fresh — zero leakage of the old thread into her opener. Back/close/stop = the thread SURVIVES and resumes; nothing clears silently. One rule, all rooms, judged as fixtures. |
| **The persona compass** | Every conversation carries the user's TYPE and its goal as her quiet agenda: ANONYMOUS → earn the saved search / account. RETURNING → earn the tour. SEASONED → the next rung (next tour, pre-qual, the consult). The judge scores every walk against the goal, not just politeness. The rungs are data (§3g). |
| **The hands** (#256.35) | Pills ARE tools: one registry, two faces (she RUNS it or OFFERS it), executed inside the chat; she can never offer anything outside it. The registry, the state table and the slot grammar are §4 — one table, not two copies. |

### The three rooms — thin mounts, not products

| Room | Context injected | Tool emphasis | Her quiet goal |
|---|---|---|---|

A mount is about a screen of code: context assembler + the shared chat UI + the room's pill affordances. That is the whole build per room once the spine exists — one architecture, not three projects. The dashboard and home-value rooms are the client dashboard's Myli dock — the surface itself is the Client Dashboard book.

**Continuity is the magic trick.** She met you in the modal hunting Prairie Village. Tonight you open your dashboard: "I kept an eye on Prairie Village since this morning — that Mission Road one you liked is still available, and something new came on at $429k. Want the quick look?" Same person. Same conversation, or a new one that REMEMBERS — her choice is deliberate, never accidental. Cross-room continuity is the feature no portal on earth has, and it costs nothing once the spine exists: the conversation id travels with the lead, and every room offers "pick up where we left off" beside "new chat."

**What dies, all three rooms:** every scripted opener · the questionnaire road ("what's your budget and how many bedrooms?") · the per-visit amnesia silo · search-history-pretending-to-be-chat-history · count-dump replies · any surface-owned sentence. If a room can't mount the loop yet, it says less — it never falls back to a script.

### 2b — The six memory laws (all signed by #256.69)

1. **PROVENANCE OR IT IS NOT A MEMORY.** `source_activity_ids` non-empty, or `created_by` an agent's explicit entry. A memory with no source is refused at the writer.
2. **TIME-AWARE FACTS.** Ages, budgets, timelines store the OBSERVATION ("about 2 years old as of 2026-03"), never a rewritten present tense; the app derives now.
3. **SUPERSEDE, NEVER DELETE.** A changed preference closes the old row (`status=superseded`, `supersedes_fact_id`) and opens a new one.
4. **EXPLICIT beats INFERRED.** Confidence bands: explicit 0.95–1.0 · agent-entered 0.95 · repeated inference 0.75–0.90 · single inference 0.40–0.65.
5. **RETRIEVAL IS RELEVANCE-BASED.** Never the whole life story in a prompt; the dog is mentioned only when the yard is.
6. **CHANNEL INDEPENDENCE IS MANDATORY.** One retrieval, one writer; a memory learned on a call is available in SMS, email, dashboard, voice. No `getSmsMemory` / `getEmailMemory` may exist (a grep cage).

## 3 — Her prompting: one small prompt per state, her context, her tools, her words

*SIGNED #257.7 · #257.8 (9/04) · #256.48–.50a (9/03)*

### 3a — The law of the prompt (#257.7, #257.8)

Zach, verbatim: "this is fucking bullshit in a goddamn prompt — chatgpt has the fucking prompt. we tell myli WHO SHE IS, WHAT THE GOAL OF THE STAGE PROMPT SHE IS IN, and THE TOOLS SHE HAS ACCESS TO." And: "they are fighting over a fucking prompt, when we are supposed to do state based prompts that are super small, like 300 characters, and we just feed myli context for each state."

**There is no one prompt. There is ONE tiny prompt PER STATE (~300 characters): who she is + the goal of THIS state + the tools THIS state can reach — and the CONTEXT for that state is fed alongside it.** A block that teaches the foundation model something it already knows is DELETED, not trimmed: the 3,291-character KC geography block went whole (it taught the model what it had read on the internet and supplied the exact fair-housing word we had banned). The test for every remaining line: does this tell her something only WE know? Only-we-know survives (the buyer's facts, our IDX truth, our tools, the stage's goal, the voice). World knowledge is deleted. The 8,000-character ceiling (#257.5) was a ceiling on a thing that should not exist as one block.

**What rides on it, already in the tree:** `PILL_STATE_TABLE` (`apps/houseloop-web/lib/myli/contract/registry.ts`) already WAS the state machine — it knew the states and what each may offer. It grew ONE authored column, `goal`; `stagePrompt(state)` composes the shared VOICE line + this state's goal (composed, never stored — a stored prompt would be the voice written down a second time); `stateToolIds(state)` DERIVES the state's tools from its own set and addons, which filters the `action` enum on the respond tool so an out-of-state pick is structurally unpickable instead of dropped after the model makes it. The 4,941-character capability menu in prose was a second copy of that enum and is gone. Every state prompt is at or under 300 characters (worst 299), measured from the real table.

### 4b — The slot grammar: structure is law, her words fill it (#256.20, revises #256.14's freestyle)
